## Scene: The Corridor Outside B6 (Movement III)

**Cyrus (MM):** You move down the quiet, carpeted gallery corridor. Ahead, a figure in steward's livery is working the lock of case B6. It's a Gallery Knife scout.
What do you do?

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa moves silently, her greatsword sheathed. She steps forward to tackle him and pin him to the wall. 'Keep quiet.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent. The corridor is warded, but you are moving fast. This is a Standard difficulty check. Dassa rolls Strength + Combat.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Social Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Scout confrontation (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Dassa, you tackle him. He grunts as you slam him to the wall, but he is quick. He slips out of his steward's coat, leaving it in your hands, and dives down the wester-gate stair. However, as he flees, a set of warded lockpicks and a parchment schematic of the gallery wards fall out of his coat pockets onto the carpet.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I'll grab the schematic and lockpicks! Pello slides over the carpet, scooping them up."

**Cyrus (MM):** Standard check. Roll Dexterity.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Social Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Scout confrontation (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Pello, you grab them, but as you do, a warded alarm crystal in the scout's coat pocket flares, humming a low warning note. The inner guards on the gallery look down the stairs. They haven't spotted you yet, but they are heading your way.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll stand in the middle of the hall, adjusting my porcelain mask. 'Minister, my cousin has simply dropped her crystal token. Let us light the sconce.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Serane, roll Wisdom + Persuade to deflect their attention. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Social Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Scout confrontation (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The guards stop. "Be careful, Lady Vaskarin. The mists are thick tonight." They turn back, but they remain stationed at the gallery entrance, preventing you from using the main stairs.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I'll study the schematic Pello grabbed. 'Let's see if the service path is warded.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Ilesse, roll Wisdom + Attune to decipher the runes. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Social Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Scout confrontation (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. You read the map. The service passage is open, but it's warded to seal automatically at midnight. You have a route, but the timer is ticking.
"Let's move," Dassa whispers. "We have what we need."

## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement II - Ballroom Networking)

**Cyrus (MM):** The music is a sweeping Orthaen string piece, beautiful but slightly hollow. Servants in dark grey coats pass trays of silver-rimmed goblets. You circulate. Serane, you want to learn about the 'Tithe of Hands'. Pello, you seek the river gate. Ilesse, you want to get word to Veier.
As you look around, you notice the first **omen**: the falconry mews outside the window are quiet, but occasionally a single hawk screams, a sharp, terrified sound that is immediately cut short. Nobody else seems to hear it.
Sophia, how does Serane approach Minister Corval's associates?

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Serane walks to the gallery railing where two junior ministers are discussing shipping records. She presents a glass of wine. 'The Boranis trade lines have been quiet, but tonight represents a return. The Merchant's Circle hears whispers of a new levy—surely it will benefit us all?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent social approach. This is a Standard difficulty check. Roll Intelligence + Persuade.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Social Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The minister nods, looking around. "The Tithe of Hands is indeed a levy, Lady Vaskarin. It requires each house to contribute to the festival laborers year-round. It is written in the private register in the Archive Room. But... my colleague here, Minister Ehran, notices your interest. He will trade this information, but he wants a favor: you must introduce him to the Vaskarin patriarch later tonight."
So you know what it is and where it is, but you've committed a social debt.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to talk to the wine cellar hands. Pello slips a Phern merchant coin to a server. 'Fine vintage. Who carries the keys to the river gate down in the gardens? My uncle wants to ensure the carters can leave early.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Pello, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Social Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The server pockets the coin. "Master Vell holds the gate key, merchant. He's at the garden pavilion. But... the butler, Corval, is looking at me. He knows I took a bribe. I'll have to shift my station to the east corridor. You'll find the guards there are doubled now."
You know who has the key, but the east corridor's security has increased.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse searches for the Nolonaire cousin. She notices a guest wearing a silver-hawk brooch. She approaches, sliding behind a stone pillar. 'The border wind blows cold, cousin.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Standard check. Roll Wisdom + Persuade.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Social Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The guest turns. "Indeed it does, Kethaun. Veier is in the east wing (B8). She is safe, but warded crystal doors block the entrance. I have a pass-key token, but it only has one charge left. I will give it to you, but you must promise to carry a sealed envelope out of this palace for me if things go wrong."
You have the pass-key, but you've taken on a mysterious task.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa grunts. 'I'll watch the guards. Let's get ready. The night is winding down.'"

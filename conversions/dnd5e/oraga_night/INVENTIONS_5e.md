# Oraga Night 5e — Inventions for Owner Review

Every new fictional fact (and every mechanical choice that reads like one) the 5e
edition had to make. Nothing here is settled until the owner approves, amends, or
strikes it. One table, numbered once; rows that several drafters logged separately
for the same fact are merged, with every file that uses the fact listed.

## Owner Rulings (2026-09-28)

These are settled, and the rows below follow them.

| # | Ruling |
|---|---|
| R1 | **One session, the night itself.** The party starts in the street, approaching the palace; the prelude and the aftermath wing are cut or reduced to MM background. Target runtime 4½–5 hours at a four-player table. |
| R2 | **Four players is the design baseline.** Every fight is balanced for four player characters, with three and five as scaling lines. Later the same day the owner set the party at **4th level** (the night ends at 5th), so every card is now built for four 4th-level characters. |
| R3 | **Knocked-out characters come back.** *Down, Not Out*: the Uninvited's drops are Stable, anyone can haul them up with an action, and the crowd does it within two rounds. |
| R4 | **Against the Uninvited, creativity beats force.** *Buying Time*: Delay, earned by clever play, not by damage. |
| R5 | **The gate fight gets a battlefield**: the wicket, the gate-walk, the parley at the grille; morale "or"; the fire clock only on idle rounds; the last bell after the gate. |
| R6 | **The heir stays secret.** No faction, NPC or clock learns of the pregnancy or the child unless a player character discovers it and tells them. (This closes the old review item #9.) |

The owner also added **the Attendant** the same day: a servant of the Uninvited's
master who came through with them. It is owner canon; its mechanics are logged at
#55.

# Playtest Scenario: Oraga Night masquerade

This document outlines the setup, player configurations, and focus areas for the ten playtests of the *Oraga Night* masquerade adventure module.

---

## Adventure Background & Premise

* **Setting:** Rekuzan, capital of the Orthaen, continent of Val'loh — 3164 PG
* **Module:** *Oraga Night* (A masquerade ball at House Boranis that culminates in a sudden midnight attack by the Uninvited).
* **Atmosphere:** Glittering masquerade (Movements I-V) followed by high-tension escape and survival (Movements VI-VII).

---

## Playtester Personas

To test different styles of play and refereeing, we use a mix of four player personas and two Mirror Master (MM) personas:

### GMs (Mirror Masters)
1.  **Arthur (Novice MM):** Rigidly relies on the rulebook, struggles with freeform magic and simultaneous combat exchanges, often reverts to traditional turn order, and interprets 2d6 partial successes (7-9) as flat penalties.
2.  **Cyrus (Expert MM):** Immersive, rules-fluent, and narrative-first. He utilizes dramatic partial successes, runs simultaneous combat exchanges smoothly, rotates the spotlight, and adjudicates freeform magic dynamically based on scope and intent.

### Players
1.  **Luke (Expert Player):** Plays **Dassa** (the ungifted House-Blade). Focuses on tactical combat, positioning, and protecting squishy allies.
2.  **Sophia (Expert Player):** Plays **Serane Vaskarin** (the Minor Scion). Leverages social heraldry, lore, and smart crystal spellcraft, managing her Sparks meticulously.
3.  **Toby (Novice Player):** Plays **Ilesse Kethaun** (the Border Cousin). Rules-hesitant, tends to hoard Sparks, and relies on basic, direct actions rather than freeform magic.
4.  **Penny (Novice Player):** Plays **Pello** (the Factor's Nephew). A chaotic Phern thief who underutilizes postures and has suboptimal Spark economy, but roleplays enthusiastically.

## Round 1 Playtest Configurations

### Run 1: The Gates of Boranis (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movements I-II (Arrival & Entrance).
* **Focus:** How a novice GM handles the gate entrance, custom lineage rules, and character introductions.
* **Objective:** Arthur gets confused by the non-binary 2d6 results and asks for traditional initiative checks on social interactions.

### Run 2: Whispers in the Crystal Court (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement II-III (Ballroom Networking).
* **Focus:** Agenda completion for Pello (Circle's Reckoning) and Serane (A House's Long Game).
* **Objective:** Arthur struggles with sandbox layout, trying to steer players into rigid dialogue paths, leading to friction.

### Run 3: Summoned to the Audience Hall (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement III (The Summons).
* **Focus:** The players are called to the Audience Hall by Raunu Boranis.
* **Objective:** Arthur runs Raunu as a traditional quest-giver, penalizing players with flat -1 modifiers on failed or partial persuasion checks.

### Run 4: Midnight Unmasking & Panic (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VI (The Unmasking & Attack).
* **Focus:** The sudden darkness, panic in the court, and initial battle against the Hollow.
* **Objective:** Arthur tries to run combat using standard turn-based initiative, creating mechanical drag and causing a TPK/high casualties as players are overwhelmed by TR 7.

### Run 5: The East Wing Retreat (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VII (Escape to River Gate).
* **Focus:** Protecting the pregnant bride Veier and the newborn in the east wing.
* **Objective:** Arthur restricts Sophia's freeform magic cast, forcing her to stick to basic actions, leading to a tragic, chaotic escape.

### Run 6: Arrival at the Palace of Secrets (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movements I-II (Arrival).
* **Focus:** Immersive description of the Crystal Court and the Weaver runes.
* **Objective:** Cyrus uses partial successes to weave interesting narrative costs (e.g., losing a crystal charge) that advance the story naturally.

### Run 7: The Dance of Agendas (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movements II-III (Social Agendas).
* **Focus:** Resolving Ilesse's Cousin's Errand and Dassa's Vanished Servant.
* **Objective:** Cyrus rotates the spotlight dynamically and introduces subtle omens (the screaming falcons, the strange language of the guests) without calling them out directly.

### Run 8: Summoned by the Recluse (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement III-IV (Summons & Finding the Root).
* **Focus:** Summit with Raunu Boranis and discovering the Root of the House (Undercurrent A).
* **Objective:** Cyrus roleplays Raunu's complete preparedness, giving the players a standard difficulty to read Boranis wards.

### Run 9: Battle in the Shadowed Court (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VI (The Unmasking & Ballroom Combat).
* **Focus:** Defensive posture blocking, aggressive posture strikes against the Hollow and the Wept.
* **Objective:** Cyrus runs simultaneous exchanges where character attacks, reactions, and enemy strikes occur at once, showing high mechanical fluency.

### Run 10: The Last Exit (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VII (East Wing Escape & River Gate).
* **Focus:** Moving through service tunnels, activating crystal barriers, and using the Wept's *Fracture* (Sorrow).
* **Objective:** A fully optimized run where the experts spend Sparks, use defensive postures, trigger the Fracture successfully to halt the Wept, and achieve a historic break by saving both Raunu and Veier.

## Round 2 Playtest Configurations (The Thief Crew Expansion)

This round introduces **Tavva's crew** (Tavva, TR 9 named thief; Gallery Knives, TR 3 mooks). Unlike the undefeatable Uninvited, Tavva's crew represents a winnable mechanical encounter that players can capture or defeat.

### Run 11: Undercover Stewards (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement II (Networking).
* **Focus:** Pello attempts to trace the trophy gallery wester-gate key.
* **Objective:** Tavva is spotted in steward's disguise. Arthur struggles to resolve Pello's Finesse/Stealth check, turning it into a rigid binary lock.

### Run 12: Scout in the Corridor (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement III (Audience Hall Corridor).
* **Focus:** Dassa confronts a Gallery Knife scout trying to slip into B6.
* **Objective:** Arthur struggles to balance the scout's escape check, setting standard difficulty but applying flat penalties on a partial success.

### Run 13: Sighting the Looters (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement IV (The Toast).
* **Focus:** Spotting Tavva's thieves stealing masks from passed-out nobles.
* **Objective:** Arthur relies on rigid rules for detection, refusing to let the players use creative attunement to listen to the shadows.

### Run 14: Chaos at the Gates (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VI (Ballroom Combat).
* **Focus:** Facing the Hollow and two Gallery Knives looters.
* **Objective:** Arthur tries to manage multiple enemies with sequential initiative turns, creating a highly congested and slow resolution path.

### Run 15: Confrontation in the Gallery (Arthur - Novice MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VII (Trophy Gallery B6).
* **Focus:** Dassa and Pello corner Tavva as she tries to steal the soul-crystal.
* **Objective:** Arthur limits Pello's action options, rejecting a creative use of Phern danger-sense to anticipate Tavva's *Vanisher* technique. Tavva escapes.

### Run 16: The steward's Mistake (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement II (Networking).
* **Focus:** Spotting Tavva in steward's disguise.
* **Objective:** Cyrus descriptions are rich; Pello's partial success reveals Tavva's route but doubles the guards in the warded gallery.

### Run 17: Confronting the Scout (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement III (Corridor B6).
* **Focus:** Intercepting a Gallery Knife scout.
* **Objective:** Cyrus uses a partial success to create a compromise—the scout escapes but drops his lockpick and a warded schematic.

### Run 18: Attuning to the Dark (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement IV (The Toast).
* **Focus:** Checking the warded gallery corridor.
* **Objective:** Ilesse rolls an Attune success. Cyrus describes the ticking Threat Clock and allows the player to deduce Tavva's plans.

### Run 19: Looting the Dead (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VI (Ballroom Combat).
* **Focus:** Fighting two Gallery Knives.
* **Objective:** Cyrus manages simultaneous exchanges, parries, and strikes cleanly, demonstrating how to deal with mooks.

### Run 20: Cornering the Master Thief (Cyrus - Expert MM)
* **Pacing:** Movement VII (Trophy Gallery B6).
* **Focus:** Confronting Tavva and her crew.
* **Objective:** Fully optimized play. The party corners Tavva, parries her escape strike, spends Sparks, and defeats/captures her (Resolve 0). Cyrus handles this as a clean, winnable victory with aftermath impact.

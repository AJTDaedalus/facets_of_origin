*   **Mirror Master (MM):** Arthur (Novice)
*   **Players:**
    *   Sophia (Expert) playing **Serane Vaskarin** (Mind-focused Scion)

    *   Toby (Novice) playing **Ilesse Kethaun** (Wisdom-focused Investigator)

## Scene: The Corridor Outside B6 (Movement III)

**Arthur (MM):** You are walking down the corridor toward the warded gallery. You see a figure in steward's livery standing by the lock of the door to B6. It's a Gallery Knife scout trying to lock the door.
What do you do?

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa lunges forward to grab his shoulder before he can turn the key. 'Stop right there, steward.'"

**Arthur (MM):** This is a Hard check (-1 modifier) because the corridor is dark and warded. Roll Strength.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Social Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Tackle check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 4) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. Failure. You slip on the polished stone, missing him completely. You crash into the warded door, taking 1 Endurance damage from the ward-spark.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I'll try to tackle him as he runs past Dassa!"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1). Roll Dexterity.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Social Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Tackle check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. Failure. Pello trips over Dassa's leg and falls flat on his face. You get the Prone condition.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll try to use my light-crystal to flare and blind him!"

**Arthur (MM):** Warding check. Hard difficulty (-1). Roll Mind.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Social Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Flash check (Hard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 1 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. Failure. Your light-crystal gutters in the dark and goes out. It's spent. You lose the charge.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I'll yell down the hall to alert the guards, forcing him to surrender!"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1). Roll Wisdom.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Social Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Alert check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6! That's four failures in a row. You yell, but the sound is swallowed by the thick corridor carpets. The scout turns the key, locking the door to B6, and shadow-steps down the back stairs. The corridor is locked, and the 'Sect Suspicion' Threat Clock advances to 2/4.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "That was extremely punishing. We couldn't get a single success because of the Hard modifier."

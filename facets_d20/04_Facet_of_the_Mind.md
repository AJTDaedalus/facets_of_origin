# Facet of the Mind

Wizards, investigators, loremasters, tinkers. People who solve problems by understanding them first, and sometimes instead.

Mind has the smallest hit die and the lightest armor, and the most ways to be useful. Its magic tradition is **Thaumaturgy**: scholarly, written down, worked out with Intelligence. But you aren't a caster because you're Mind. You're a caster because you took the *Thaumaturgy* talent. A Mind character who never does is an investigator, a physician or a duellist with a notebook, and this chapter makes sure that character can hold their own in a fight.

Zahna is Mind. He would like you to know that the rest of the party is not.

### Studied Eye

You look at a creature the way other people read a page. As a bonus action, study a creature you can see within 60 feet. It becomes your **studied target** until the fight ends or you study a different creature. You learn its damage resistances, immunities and vulnerabilities, and your next attack roll against it this turn has advantage.

You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain every use when you finish a short or long rest. Several Mind talents work on your studied target.

## Preset Classes

> **Reading the cards**
>
> **Abilities** lists your scores in priority order, for the standard array. **Background** suggests one from Chapter 06 and an **origin talent**, which can come from any Facet. **Level 1** is your two starting talents. **Domains** is for casters (Chapter 07). **Picks** are the recommended talent at each level to 5th, then a line for the rest of the way. **Signature** is chosen at 3rd level and doesn't cost a pick. **Kit** is drawn from the Mind kit in Chapter 02.

### Wizard

*"I am a student of the rules underneath the world, and I have found some loopholes."*

**Abilities:** Intelligence, Constitution, Dexterity

**Background:** Guild Apprentice, origin talent *Tough* (from the Body menu; a d6 hit die needs it)

**Level 1:** *Thaumaturgy*, *Studied Recovery*

**Domains:** Constructed Force and Warding

**Picks:** 2nd *Expertise* (Arcana and History) · 3rd *Careful Casting* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Wider Study* (Illusion)

**Later:** *Empowered Spell*, *Potent Cantrips*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Contingency Plan*, *Iron Mind*

**Signature:** *Arcane Mastery*

**Kit:** Leather armor, a quarterstaff, a dagger, a scholar's pack, a spellcasting focus

**Play it like:** you are the solution to the problem nobody else can reach. Stand at the back and pick the moment.

### Investigator

*"I am the one who noticed. Then I am the one who acts on it."*

**Abilities:** Dexterity, Intelligence, Constitution

**Background:** Physician's Assistant, origin talent *Skilled*

**Level 1:** *Martial Training*, *Exploit Weakness*

**Picks:** 2nd *Expertise* · 3rd *Anticipate* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Extra Attack*

**Later:** *Decisive Strike*, *Cunning Action* (cross-Facet, Body), *Ability Score Improvement*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Iron Mind*

**Signature:** *Anatomist*

**Kit:** Leather armor, a light crossbow and 20 bolts, a dagger, a scholar's pack, a set of artisan's tools. Buy a rapier with your first reward; *Martial Training* lets you use it.

**Play it like:** study first, then one precise hit. You never cast a spell, and you never need to.

### Tier 1

**Ability Score Improvement** *(tier 1)*

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each. No score can go above 20. You can take this talent any number of times.

**Expertise** *(tier 1, origin)*

Choose two skills you are proficient in. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make with them.

**Exploit Weakness** *(tier 1)*

Once per turn, when you hit your studied target with a weapon attack, you deal extra damage: **1d6 for every two levels you have, rounded up** (1d6 at 1st, 3d6 at 5th, 5d6 at 9th). You can't use it on a turn in which you make more than one attack, counting bonus-action attacks, and it never combines with the Body talent *Sneak Attack*.

**Field Medic** *(tier 1, origin)*

As an action, you can use a healer's kit on a creature within reach. It regains hit points equal to 1d6 + your proficiency bonus + your level. A creature can benefit from this once per short rest. You can also stabilize a dying creature as a bonus action.

**Gadgeteer** *(tier 1, origin)*

When you finish a long rest with tinker's tools to hand, you build a number of gadgets equal to your proficiency bonus, chosen from Table 4–3. Using a gadget takes an action unless the table says otherwise. Each works once, and any you haven't used fall apart when you next finish a long rest. The save DC is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier.

**Table 4–3: Gadgets**

| Gadget | What it does |
|---|---|
| Flash-pot | Throw it up to 30 feet. Each creature within 5 feet of where it lands makes a Constitution save or is blinded until the end of its next turn. |
| Smoke-pot | Throw it up to 30 feet. A 10-foot-radius cloud of smoke fills the area, heavily obscured, for 1 minute or until a strong wind clears it. |
| Grapnel | Fire a line up to 60 feet to a surface that can hold it. Climbing the line costs you no extra movement. |
| Tripwire | Set it across a gap up to 20 feet wide. The first creature to cross it sets off a bell that can be heard 300 feet away. Setting it takes a minute. |
| Spark-lighter | A 15-foot cone of sparks. Each creature in it makes a Dexterity save, taking 2d6 fire damage (3d6 from 5th level) on a failure or half on a success. |

**Magic Initiate** *(tier 1, origin)*

Choose Thaumaturgy or Invocation, and one of its domains (Chapter 07). You learn two cantrips from that domain and one 1st-level spell from it. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and can't do so again until you finish a long rest; you can also cast it with any slot you have. Your spellcasting ability is Intelligence for Thaumaturgy, and Wisdom or Charisma (your choice) for Invocation. This doesn't make you a caster, and gives you no slots.

**Martial Training** *(tier 1)*

You gain proficiency with martial weapons, and training with medium armor and shields.

**Skilled** *(tier 1, origin)*

You gain proficiency in any combination of three skills or tools. You can take this talent more than once.

**Studied Recovery** *(tier 1)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy*

Once per long rest, when you finish a short rest, you can recover spent spell slots whose levels add up to no more than half your level, rounded up. None of them can be 5th level.

**Thaumaturgy** *(tier 1)*

You are a caster of the scholarly tradition, and cast with **Intelligence**. Choose **two Thaumaturgy domains** from Chapter 07. You know two cantrips from their lists (three from 4th level, four at 10th), and you prepare Intelligence modifier + your caster level spells from their lists after each long rest. Your spell slots come from the **Full** table in Chapter 07, read by your **caster level**: the number of levels you have held this talent, counting the level you took it as 1. Take it at 1st level and that is simply your level.

A character of another Facet can take *Thaumaturgy* as a cross-Facet pick from 2nd level. They become a **half caster** instead, as Chapter 07 describes. It can't be an origin talent.

**Well-Timed Word** *(tier 1)*

When a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, an ability check or a damage roll, you can use your reaction to say exactly the wrong thing at exactly the right time. Roll a d6 (a d8 from 5th level) and subtract the number from the roll. You can do this a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once), and regain every use when you finish a short or long rest.

### Tier 2 (from 3rd level)

**Anticipate** *(tier 2)*

When your studied target makes an attack roll against you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you, you can use your reaction to give the roll disadvantage. There's no limit on how often, beyond the one reaction you get each round. You've watched this creature long enough to know where it's going.

**Careful Casting** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy*

When you cast a spell that affects an area, choose a number of creatures in it up to 1 + the spell's level. They succeed on their saving throws against it automatically, and take no damage from it.

**Extra Attack** *(tier 2, 5th level)*

**Prerequisite:** *Martial Training*

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you attack twice instead of once.

**Infuse Item** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy*

When you finish a long rest, you can infuse up to two nonmagical objects you touch. Each becomes one of these: a **+1 weapon** (+1 to attack and damage rolls), a **+1 armor or shield** (+1 AC), or a **stored-spell trinket** holding one 1st-level spell you have prepared, which whoever holds it can cast once using your spell save DC and attack bonus. Your infusions last until you infuse again.

**Potent Cantrips** *(tier 2, 7th level)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy* or *Invocation*

When you cast a cantrip that deals damage, add your spellcasting modifier to one of its damage rolls.

**Wider Study** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy* or *Invocation*

Choose a third domain from a tradition you hold. Its spells join the lists you prepare from.

### Tier 3 (from 6th level)

**Contingency Plan** *(tier 3)*

When you finish a long rest, you can describe a trigger ("the moment someone draws steel in the hall") and one action you'll take when it happens. A caster can name a spell of 3rd level or lower that they have prepared. If the trigger happens within the next 24 hours, you take the action at once, as a reaction, spending any slot then. You can have one plan at a time.

**Decisive Strike** *(tier 3)*

**Prerequisite:** *Exploit Weakness*

When you hit your studied target with an attack, you can turn the hit into a critical hit. Once you do, you can't again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Empowered Spell** *(tier 3)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy*

When you cast a spell that deals damage, you can deal the maximum on every damage die instead of rolling. Once you do, you can't again until you finish a long rest.

**Iron Mind** *(tier 3)*

You gain proficiency in Constitution, Dexterity or Charisma saving throws (choose one), and you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed.

## Mind Signatures

At 3rd level, choose one. It costs no talent pick, you get only one, and you can't change it later. A signature that needs a talent says so.

**Arcane Mastery**

**Requires:** *Thaumaturgy*

Choose two 1st-level spells and one 2nd-level spell from your domains' lists. They are always prepared, and don't count against the number you can prepare. Once per short rest, you can cast one of them at its lowest level without spending a slot.

**Master Plan**

Once per long rest, before a fight you had at least a scene to prepare for, lay out the plan to the table. Your side goes first unless it is surprised, and during the first round you and every ally who follows the plan have advantage on attack rolls. (The MM decides whether an ally is following it. If they are arguing about it, they aren't.)

**Encyclopedic**

Once per long rest, state a fact about a creature, a place or a piece of history. It is true. The MM may add a complication, but can't make the fact false. If the fact concerns a creature, you and every ally who heard you have advantage on your first saving throw against it.

**Clockwork Companion**

You build a small construct: AC 13, hit points equal to 5 × your level, speed 30 feet, immune to poison damage and the poisoned condition. It moves on its own on your turn, but acts only when you spend a bonus action to command it: it takes the Help or Dodge action, or makes a slam attack using your Intelligence modifier + proficiency bonus to hit, dealing 1d8 + your proficiency bonus bludgeoning damage. If it is destroyed, you can rebuild it when you finish a long rest.

**Anatomist**

Your attacks against your studied target score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. When you score one, choose one: the target is knocked prone, or its speed is 0 until the end of its next turn, or it has disadvantage on its next attack roll.

**Fortress of Thought**

You have advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws. Nothing can read your thoughts or change your memories unless you allow it.

## Building a Mind Character

Your first decision on this menu is whether to take *Thaumaturgy*. It costs one of your two starting talents, and it's worth it if you want to cast. If you don't, the other ten tier 1 talents are waiting.

> **Through the Mirror — the Mind character who never casts**
>
> The rule we held ourselves to: a Mind character who never casts should be as good in a fight as one who does. The Investigator is the test. At 1st level, a rapier and *Exploit Weakness* against a studied target deal about what an SRD rogue deals. At 5th, they deal about what an SRD rogue deals. The wizard beside them has spells, and the Investigator has *Martial Training*, medium armor and a shield. Neither is carrying the other.

### A Custom Build: the Physician

A player wants a battlefield surgeon who has never cast a spell and doesn't plan to. There's no card for it. At 1st level: *Field Medic* and *Expertise* (Medicine and Insight). The Physician's Assistant background gives *Skilled* as the origin talent: herbalism kit, Nature, Survival.

At 2nd level, *Martial Training*, for a shield and something heavier than a stick. At 3rd, *Anticipate*: the surgeon who knows where the blow will land. The signature is *Master Plan*, because a surgeon plans. By 5th level this character can patch up every friend once between short rests, keeps them out of trouble with *Anticipate*, and swings a longsword they are properly trained with. It doesn't look like any SRD class, and it doesn't have to.

### A Cross-Facet Build: the Scholar-Priest

A Mind character who studies the gods as a scholar, and finds that some of them answer. At 1st level, *Thaumaturgy* (Divination and Warding) and *Studied Recovery*. At 2nd level, *Invocation* as a cross-Facet pick, choosing Wisdom and The Tide and Presence.

A character never has two slot tables. The scholar-priest keeps the Full table they already have and adds two Invocation domains to the lists they prepare from. Invocation spells they prepare use Wisdom; Thaumaturgy spells use Intelligence. They pay for it with a talent pick and a second ability to keep up. What they get is the widest spell list in the game, and a lot of arguments with the Priest about who's right.

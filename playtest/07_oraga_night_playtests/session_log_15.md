## Scene: The Trophy Gallery B6 (Movement VII - Escape)

**Arthur (MM):** You run into the Trophy Gallery. The room is filled with warded glass cases. You see Tavva (TR 9) standing before the Boranis family lattice, holding the Vaskarin soul-crystal. She sees you.
"I told you I'm just here for the cargo," she says. "Let me pass."
Let's enter combat. Declare your postures. Dassa is Defensive. Pello is Measured.
Dassa, Pello, what do you do?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to run up and strike her hand to knock the soul-crystal loose!"
>
> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa lunges with a sweeping greatsword strike to block her path."

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, both strike. Let's roll Pello's strike. Standard difficulty. Roll Dexterity.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Strike Tavva]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Pello, you hit her hand, but she holds onto the crystal. She attacks you back with a swift counter-slash.
Now Dassa's strike. Roll Strength + Combat. With Defensive posture penalty, +1 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Strike Tavva]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 4) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 8. Another partial success. Dassa, your blade cuts her shoulder. Tavva takes a strike (2 damage), dropping to 1 Resolve. But she lashes back at you too.
Now you both must react. Pello, you Dodge. Dassa, you Parry.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello React Tavva]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Dodge roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 1 = **6** (Failure).

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa React Tavva]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Parry roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 2) + 2 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** Both are failures! A 6 for Pello and a 6 for Dassa.
Tavva's counter-slash hits Pello, dealing 2 damage. Pello's Endurance drops from 3 to 1.
Dassa, you parry too late. Tavva hits you, but your heavy armor absorbs it. You spend your last armor charge.
And then, Tavva triggers her **Vanisher** technique! A warded dark-burst crystal explodes, filling the gallery with pitch-black shadow.

> **Penny (Pello):** "Wait! I have my Phern danger-sense! I want to roll Finesse to feel where she is moving in the dark and tackle her before she escapes!"
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "No, the dark-burst crystal is magical. Your danger-sense only warns you of attacks, it doesn't let you see in magical darkness. You can't roll. Tavva and her crew are gone by the next exchange, along with the Vaskarin soul-crystal. You are left standing in the dark."
>
> **Sophia (Serane):** "Darn. The Vanisher technique is extremely powerful if GMs don't allow creative player counters. We lost the crystal."

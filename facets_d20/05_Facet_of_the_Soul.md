# Facet of the Soul

The Soul is the Facet of people who work through what they believe and who stands with them: the priest at a sickbed, the druid who was a wolf a minute ago, the oracle who said this would happen, the oathsworn knight who won't step aside. Its tradition is **Invocation**, the intuitive magic, cast with whichever of Wisdom and Charisma is higher. Its menu holds more Spell talents than any other, and the Priest, the Druid and the Oracle all come out of it. They differ only in the picks.

## Soul Feature

### Kindle
*Soul feature · 1st level*

As a bonus action, a creature within 60 feet that can hear you, or you yourself, gains a Spark (it still can't hold more than 3). You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus; all uses return on a long rest.

Two Steel talents, six Spell talents and four general ones. To read an entry, see *Reading a talent entry* in Chapter 02.

### Channel
*Spell talent · Soul*

As a Magic action, choose a creature within 30 feet. It regains 1d8 + your casting modifier hit points, or it makes a Constitution save against your spell save DC and takes that much radiant or necrotic damage (half on a success). You have one use; it returns on a short rest.

Also, when you cast a spell with a slot and it restores hit points as you cast it, one creature it heals regains extra hit points equal to 2 + the slot's level.

**5th (Spell 2):** two uses, one of which returns on a short rest; 2d8 instead of 1d8.
**9th (Spell 3):** three uses, one of which returns on a short rest; 3d8 instead.

### Wild Shape
*Spell talent · Soul*

As a bonus action, become a beast of CR 1/4 or lower that has no flying speed, for the rest of the fight or up to an hour. You gain temporary hit points equal to your level, keep your talents and Facet features, and can't cast spells. You have two uses; one returns on a short rest, both on a long rest.

**5th (Spell 2):** a beast of CR 1/2 or lower.
**9th (Spell 3):** a beast of CR 1 or lower, and it can have a flying speed.

### Mending Hands
*Spell talent · Soul*

You have a pool of healing equal to 5 × your level, refilled on a long rest. As a bonus action, touch a creature and restore any number of hit points from the pool, or spend 5 from it to end the poisoned condition.

### Prophecy
*Spell talent · Soul*

After a long rest, speak one sentence about the day ahead: something you can't simply make happen yourself (the MM can veto a trivial one). The first time the MM agrees it has come true, each ally who heard it gains a Spark, once per ally per day.

**5th (Spell 2):** when it comes true, you also regain every use of *Kindle*.

### Sworn Strike
*Steel talent · Soul*

Once per turn, when you hit with a melee weapon, you can spend a use to deal an extra 2d8 radiant damage. You have uses equal to your Soul modifier (minimum 1) per long rest, and one returns on a short rest. This is a rider. It is the only way in the game to spend a resource for extra damage on a weapon hit; there are no smite spells on the lists (Chapter 07). Your **Soul modifier** is the higher of your Wisdom and Charisma modifiers, whatever your Facet.

**5th (Steel 2):** 3d8 instead of 2d8.
**9th (Steel 3):** 4d8 instead. On a turn you hit with a melee weapon and spend no use, add 1d8 radiant instead. Either way it is a rider.

### Warden
*General talent · Soul*

While you are conscious, you and allies within 10 feet add +1 to saving throws.

**5th:** the bonus is your Soul modifier (the higher of your Wisdom and Charisma modifiers, minimum +1) instead.
**9th:** you can't be frightened.

### Oath Unbroken
*General talent · Soul*

Speak an oath of one sentence; it becomes one of your Drives. While you keep it, once per long rest, when you would drop to 0 hit points you drop to 1 instead, and your next weapon or spell hit in that fight deals maximum damage. If you break the oath, this talent does nothing until you make amends.

## One Menu, Four Presets

The Soul presets show how far the picks alone move a character. All four start from the same d8, the same saves and the same *Kindle*.

**Table 5–3: How the Soul Presets Differ**

| Preset | Spell talents | Other picks | First domain, then more | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priest | *Channel*, *Wider Study*, *Mending Hands* | *Warden*, *Hardy* | The Tide; Presence, The Living World | pure caster |
| Druid | *Wild Shape*, *Wider Study*, *Channel* | *Hardy*, *Warden* | Verdance; The Tide, The Living World | pure caster |
| Oracle | *Turn the Odds*, *Wider Study*, *Prophecy* | *Warden*, *Alert* | Presence; Binding, Fate | pure caster |
| Oathsworn | *Mending Hands* | *Sworn Strike*, *Weapon Expert*, *Warden*, *Oath Unbroken* | Presence | hybrid, Steel main |

All three pure casters reach a prismatic domain at 5th, when the third Spell talent brings Deep Magic: the Priest and the Druid take The Living World, and the Oracle, which starts on Presence and adds Binding at 3rd, comes into Fate.

    Create Your Character

    Four choices and you're playing: a name, a Facet, a Background, and how your nine Attributes are rated. Nothing here is permanent — advancement changes all of it.

    This Background grants a magic domain — choose one, or leave it blank for the non-magical variant. Until you unlock a Technique your magic works at Minor scope only; there is no extra difficulty on top of that.
    Distribute Attributes

    Rate each Attribute 1 to 3. Spend every point — 2 is competent, 3 is exceptional, 1 is a genuine weakness worth playing.

    Actions
    Strike

    Against an enemy a 10+ depletes 2 Resolve and may hang a Condition; a 7-9 depletes 1. Against another character a 10+ is a Tier 2 Condition and a 7-9 is Tier 1.

    Sparks staged on your sheet above apply to this Strike too.
    React

    One reaction per incoming action. At 0 Endurance only Absorb is available.

    Maneuver

    Change the situation instead of dealing damage — disarm, shove, blind, cut the rope. A Maneuver is defined by what you describe.

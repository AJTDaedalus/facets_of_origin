## Facets (Optional Modules)
The following content is not part of the core rules. Each is a standalone Facet module a Mirror Master can add to any campaign.

- **Downtime**: training, research, recovery and personal projects between adventures
- **Crafting**: making equipment, draughts and magical items
- **Economy**: trade, commerce and resource management
- **Feats**: extra options for characters beyond the core talent menus
- **Technology**: technological settings and equipment
- **Shattered Origin (setting Facet)**: the full setting, with expanded lore, lineages and setting-specific content

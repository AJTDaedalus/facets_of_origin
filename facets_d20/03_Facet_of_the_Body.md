# Facet of the Body

Fighters, rogues, barbarians, monks. People who solve problems by being in the right place with the right amount of force, or the right amount of quiet.

Body has no magic tradition of its own, and it doesn't miss one. In exchange it gets the biggest hit die, every armor and weapon, a third talent at 1st level, a way to get back on its feet in the middle of a fight, and Extra Attack at 5th level without spending a pick on it. A Body character who wants a few spells can learn them from another Facet (see *Building a Body Character*), but nothing in this chapter asks you to.

Mordai is Body. So is Zulnut. If one Facet can hold both of them, it can hold whatever you had in mind.

## Body at a Glance

**Table 3–1: The Body Facet**

| | |
|---|---|
| Hit die | d10 |
| Hit points at 1st level | 10 + Constitution modifier |
| Hit points each level after | 6 + Constitution modifier |
| Saving throws | Strength, Constitution |
| Armor training | All armor, shields |
| Weapon proficiencies | Simple and martial weapons |
| Skills | Three from Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Survival |
| Talents at 1st level | Three tier 1 talents from the Body menu |
| Facet feature | *Second Wind* at 1st level; *Extra Attack* at 5th |
| Spellcasting | None of its own. Invocation or Thaumaturgy as a cross-Facet pick from 2nd level makes you a half caster (Chapter 07). |

### Second Wind

You have a reserve most people never find. As a bonus action, regain hit points equal to **1d10 + your level**. You can do this twice, three times from 5th level. You regain one use when you finish a short rest and all of them when you finish a long rest.

### Extra Attack

From 5th level, when you take the Attack action on your turn, you attack twice instead of once. Every Body character gets this. It doesn't cost a talent.

## Preset Classes

A preset is a build somebody already worked out: which talents to take and when, which signature to aim for, what to carry. Take one as printed and you are ready to play. Swap any pick for another legal one and it's still yours. Nothing here is a class in the SRD sense; there is no Fighter feature, only Body talents a fighter tends to take.

> **Reading the cards**
>
> **Abilities** lists your scores in priority order, for the standard array. **Background** suggests one from Chapter 06 and an **origin talent** to take with it. **Level 1** is your three starting talents. **Picks** are the recommended talent at each level to 5th, then a line for the rest of the way. **Signature** is chosen at 3rd level and doesn't cost a pick. **Kit** is drawn from the Body kit in Chapter 02.

### Fighter

*"I am a trained soldier, and the other side runs out of fight before I do."*

**Abilities:** Strength, Constitution, Dexterity

**Background:** City Watch Veteran, origin talent *Alert*

**Level 1:** *Fighting Style* (Defense or Dueling), *Weapon Mastery*, *Tough*

**Picks:** 2nd a second *Fighting Style* · 3rd *Action Surge* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Expertise* (Athletics and Intimidation)

**Later:** *Ability Score Improvement*, *Indomitable*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Cleaving Blow*, *Survivor*

**Signature:** *Champion's Edge*

**Kit:** Chain mail, a longsword and a shield, an explorer's pack

**Play it like:** the person who walks in first and out last, and has an opinion about everyone's footwork.

### Rogue

*"I am the one who gets in, gets it done, and is somewhere else when anyone looks."*

**Abilities:** Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence

**Background:** Dockworker, origin talent *Tough*

**Level 1:** *Sneak Attack*, *Expertise*, *Cunning Action*

**Picks:** 2nd *Weapon Mastery* · 3rd *Uncanny Dodge* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Evasion*

**Later:** *Reliable Talent*, *Alert*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Indomitable*, *Ability Score Improvement*

**Signature:** *Ambush*

**Kit:** Leather armor, a longbow and 20 arrows, a rapier and a shortsword, an explorer's pack

**Play it like:** one precise hit a turn, then gone. From 5th level, choose each turn between *Sneak Attack* and your two ordinary attacks.

### Barbarian

*"I am a storm that someone taught to hold a weapon."*

**Abilities:** Strength, Constitution, Dexterity

**Background:** Wilderness Scout, origin talent *Alert*

**Level 1:** *Rage*, *Unarmored Defense* (Constitution), *Reckless Attack*

**Picks:** 2nd *Weapon Mastery* · 3rd *Relentless Rage* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Ability Score Improvement*

**Later:** *Survivor*, *Tough*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Cleaving Blow*, *Indomitable*

**Signature:** *Fury*

**Kit:** Leather armor (for travel), a longbow and 20 arrows, a greataxe and a flail, an explorer's pack

**Play it like:** you are the answer to "who do they hit first?", and you'd like it to stay that way.

### Monk

*"I am a discipline that happens to have fists."*

**Abilities:** Dexterity, Wisdom, Constitution

**Background:** Street Performer, origin talent *Tough*

**Level 1:** *Martial Arts*, *Unarmored Defense* (Wisdom), *Focus*

**Picks:** 2nd *Alert* · 3rd *Evasion* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Stunning Strike*

**Later:** *Indomitable*, *Uncanny Dodge*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Survivor*

**Signature:** *Still Water*

**Kit:** Take the 100 gp instead: a quarterstaff, ten darts, an explorer's pack, and money left over

**Play it like:** always moving, never where the enemy's plan needed you to be.

## Body Talents

Take three at 1st level, then one at every level from 2nd to 10th. Tier 1 talents are open from 1st level, tier 2 from 3rd, tier 3 from 6th. A talent marked *origin* can also be your background's origin talent, for a character of any Facet.

> **Reading the entries**
>
> The header gives the name, the tier, and *origin* if it can be an origin talent. **Prerequisite** is printed only when there is one: a talent you must already have, and never more than one. A talent may be taken once unless it says otherwise.

### Tier 1

**Ability Score Improvement** *(tier 1)*

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each. No score can go above 20. You can take this talent any number of times. It is on all three menus, so it always counts as your own Facet's.

**Alert** *(tier 1, origin)*

When you roll for your side in the initiative contest (Chapter 08), add your proficiency bonus. While you are conscious, your side can't be surprised; you shout in time.

**Cunning Action** *(tier 1, origin)*

You can take the Dash, Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action.

**Expertise** *(tier 1, origin)*

Choose two skills you are proficient in. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make with them. This talent is also on the Mind menu.

**Fighting Style** *(tier 1)*

Choose one style you don't already have. You can take this talent again for a different style.

- **Archery.** +2 to attack rolls with ranged weapons.
- **Defense.** +1 AC while you wear armor.
- **Dueling.** +2 to damage rolls with a melee weapon held in one hand, when you hold no other weapon.
- **Great Weapon.** When you roll damage for a two-handed or versatile weapon held in two hands, treat any 1 or 2 on a damage die as a 3.
- **Protection.** While you hold a shield, when a creature you can see attacks an ally within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to give the attack disadvantage.
- **Two-Weapon.** When you make the extra attack of two-weapon fighting, add your ability modifier to its damage.

**Focus** *(tier 1)*

**Prerequisite:** *Martial Arts*

You have focus points equal to your level, and regain all of them when you finish a short or long rest. Spend 1 point for one of these as a bonus action: **Flurry** (make two unarmed strikes instead of the one *Martial Arts* gives you), **Patient Defense** (take the Dodge action), or **Step of the Wind** (take the Dash and Disengage actions, and your jump distance doubles for the turn).

**Martial Arts** *(tier 1)*

While you wear no armor and hold no shield, your unarmed strikes and simple or light melee weapons use Dexterity for attack and damage if you like, and deal a d6 (d8 from 5th level) in place of their normal damage die if that's higher. After you take the Attack action, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action. Your speed also rises by 10 feet while unarmored.

**Rage** *(tier 1)*

As a bonus action, if you aren't wearing heavy armor, you rage. While raging you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage, deal +2 damage with Strength-based weapon attacks (+3 from 9th level), and have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws. You can't cast spells or concentrate on them. A rage lasts until the fight ends, you are incapacitated, or you end it. You can rage twice (three times from 3rd level, four from 6th), and regain one use on a short rest and all on a long rest.

**Reckless Attack** *(tier 1)*

When you make your first attack on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. You have advantage on melee attack rolls using Strength this turn, and attack rolls against you have advantage until the start of your next turn.

**Sneak Attack** *(tier 1)*

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a finesse or ranged weapon and you have advantage on the roll, or an ally of yours is within 5 feet of the target, you deal extra damage: **1d6 for every two levels you have, rounded up** (1d6 at 1st, 3d6 at 5th, 5d6 at 9th). You can't use it on a turn in which you make more than one attack, counting bonus-action attacks. It never combines with the Mind talent *Exploit Weakness*.

> **Through the Mirror — why one attack**
>
> Every Body character gets Extra Attack at 5th level. An SRD rogue doesn't, and its Sneak Attack is priced for one hit a turn. So we kept the SRD dice and added the choice: one careful hit that lands the dice, or two ordinary ones. At 5th level they come out close, and by 9th the careful hit is the better bet against anything worth hitting. The rogue ends up where the SRD put it, and nobody has to work out a new table.

**Tough** *(tier 1, origin)*

Your hit point maximum increases by 2 for every level you have. Each time you gain a level, it increases by 2 more.

**Unarmored Defense** *(tier 1)*

While you wear no armor, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + either your Constitution modifier (and a shield still counts) or your Wisdom modifier (and you can't use a shield). Choose Constitution or Wisdom when you take this talent. If you choose Constitution, you also have advantage on Dexterity saving throws while you wear no armor. Nothing is slowing you down.

**Weapon Mastery** *(tier 1)*

Choose three kinds of weapon you are proficient with, such as longswords, longbows and handaxes. You can use the mastery property of those weapons as the SRD 5.2.1 describes it (Cleave, Graze, Nick, Push, Sap, Slow, Topple or Vex). When you finish a long rest, you can swap one kind for another.

### Tier 2 (from 3rd level)

**Action Surge** *(tier 2)*

Once on your turn, you can take one additional action. You can't use that action to cast a spell. Once you use this talent, you can't again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Evasion** *(tier 2)*

When an effect lets you make a Dexterity saving throw to take half damage, you take no damage on a success and half on a failure. This doesn't work while you are incapacitated.

**Relentless Rage** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Rage*

If you drop to 0 hit points while raging and don't die outright, make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a success you drop to 1 hit point instead. Each time you use this, the DC rises by 5; it resets to 10 when you finish a short or long rest.

**Stunning Strike** *(tier 2, 5th level)*

**Prerequisite:** *Focus*

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike or a *Martial Arts* weapon, you can spend 1 focus point. The target makes a Constitution saving throw against DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier. On a failure it is stunned until the start of your next turn. On a success its speed is halved until then, and your next attack against it has advantage.

A boss (Chapter 08) shrugs off part of it: a stunned boss skips its next turn and the stun ends there, so it still takes its other turn.

**Uncanny Dodge** *(tier 2)*

When an attacker you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to halve the attack's damage against you.

### Tier 3 (from 6th level)

**Cleaving Blow** *(tier 3)*

Once per turn, when you score a critical hit with a melee weapon or drop a creature to 0 hit points with one, you can make one more weapon attack straight away, as part of the same action.

**Indomitable** *(tier 3)*

When you fail a saving throw, you can reroll it and add your level to the new roll. You must use the new roll. Once you use this, you can't again until you finish a long rest.

**Reliable Talent** *(tier 3)*

When you make an ability check with a skill or tool you are proficient in, treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.

**Survivor** *(tier 3)*

At the start of each of your turns, if you are Bloodied but still have at least 1 hit point, you regain 5 + your Constitution modifier hit points.

## Body Signatures

At 3rd level, choose one. A signature is the thing people remember about your character. It costs no talent pick, you get only one, and you can't change it later. A signature that needs a talent says so.

**Champion's Edge**

Your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20. Whenever you score one, you regain hit points equal to your level.

**Ambush**

In the first round of a fight, you have advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't had a turn yet, and the first time you hit each such creature you deal an extra 2d6 damage. This stacks with *Sneak Attack*.

**Fury**

**Requires:** *Rage*

While you are raging, you can't be charmed or frightened, and once per turn when you hit with a weapon attack, you roll one extra damage die of the weapon's kind.

**Still Water**

**Requires:** *Martial Arts*

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can also do one of these: push it 10 feet away from you; knock it prone unless it succeeds on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier); or stop it taking reactions until the start of its next turn.

**Bulwark**

Allies within 5 feet of you have half cover against ranged attacks: +2 to their AC. A creature you hit with an opportunity attack has its speed drop to 0 for the rest of the turn.

**Deadeye**

Your ranged weapon attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover, and attacking at long range doesn't give you disadvantage. Once per turn, when you hit with a ranged weapon, you deal extra damage equal to your proficiency bonus.

## Building a Body Character

The presets are four points on a wide map. The Body menu has thirteen tier 1 talents and you take three of them before anything else happens, so there are a lot of other characters in here. Pick talents that tell the same story and you won't go far wrong. Pick two that want the same bonus action every turn (*Cunning Action* and *Martial Arts*, say) and you've bought one talent you won't use much.

### A Custom Build: Zulnut

Zulnut's player went after Mordai's, and didn't want a preset.

**Zulnut:** Body. He's a disciple of a wandering teacher who moved on. The Monk card is close, but he doesn't do *Focus*. Focus is effort.

**MM:** Then don't take it. What does he do?

**Zulnut:** *Martial Arts*, because he'd rather not carry a weapon. *Unarmored Defense* with Wisdom, because armor is heavy. And *Cunning Action*, so he can leave.

**MM:** *Martial Arts* gives you a bonus-action punch, and *Cunning Action* gives you a bonus-action exit. You get one bonus action a turn.

**Zulnut:** Then each turn I choose. Hit twice, or hit once and leave. That's two good options for no extra effort.

**MM:** (He has found a way to make the talent list feel lazy.) Signature at 3rd?

**Zulnut:** *Still Water*. Knock them over, then walk off.

That build is as legal as any card in this chapter. It gives up the Monk's focus points for a way out of every fight, and a player who knows who their character is will usually make trades like that.

### A Cross-Facet Build: the Ranger Shape

There is no ranger card, because a ranger is a Body character who learned a little Invocation. At 1st level, take *Fighting Style* (Archery), *Weapon Mastery* and *Alert*. At 2nd level, take **Invocation** as a cross-Facet pick. That makes you a **half caster** (Chapter 07). Choose Wisdom as your casting ability and two Invocation domains; Beasts and Verdance are the woodland pair. At 3rd level take *Deadeye*.

Your caster level counts from the level you took the talent, so at 2nd level you cast as a 1st-level half caster, and you stay one step behind a character who has cast from the start. That's the price of the bow arm. You still get Extra Attack at 5th level like every Body character.

A holy warrior works the same way: Body, *Fighting Style* (Defense), and Invocation at 2nd level with Presence and Binding. If you would rather be a Soul character who fights than a Body character who prays, the Oathsworn card in Chapter 05 is the other road.

> **MM Note — cross-Facet picks**
>
> **Default:** any tier 1 talent from another Facet, from 2nd level, for one pick. Tier 2 and 3 talents from another Facet need two talents from that Facet first (Chapter 02).
>
> **The dial:** if your table likes a story for it, ask where the character learned it: a teacher, a shrine, a book. Don't make it a quest unless the player wants one.
>
> **The cost:** a player who is charged a side quest for a talent learns that building a character is a negotiation, and stops trying odd ideas.

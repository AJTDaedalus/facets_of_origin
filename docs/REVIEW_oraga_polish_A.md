*Scope: 01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 11 in `conversions/dnd5e/oraga_night/`, read in full, with other
chapters opened only to check a cross-reference. Authority: `docs/DECISIONS.md` TP1–TP46 and the TPN
notes. No module file was edited. Where a finding is already an open task in
`docs/TASKS_oraga_5e_3p_pass.md`, it is tagged **[tracked: T#]**, so it can be ticked off rather than

## Blockers

### B1. The Second Clause still sends word to the river (TP21) [tracked: T7.11] — **FIXED (07 and F/07; 05's copy by the 05/09 worker)**
- **07:771** — *"If a woman in Thenya wool comes out the front, hold her, and send word to the river."*
- TP21 cuts "and send word to the river"; the clause ends at "hold her".
- **Fix:** *"If a woman in Thenya wool comes out the front, hold her."* Also do T7.11's captain-route
  reword (07:783–787 still works as written).

### B2. The Root: "the Uninvited will not enter it" (TP8, TP18) [tracked: T7.2] — **FIXED (TP47; 04 and F/04, F/05, F/08)**
- **04:721–723** — *"And the Uninvited will not enter it: whatever the deep crystal is to them, they will
  not cross its threshold, and nothing in this adventure explains why."*
- This contradicts TP8 (they don't know to avoid it; it is a trap, not a refuge) and TP18 (nobody,
  Raunu included, knows the effect). It also contradicts chapter V's own ⟨They trap one of the
  Uninvited⟩ (05:1308, "lures one into the Root's threshold") and Table V–6's note that the trap is an
  End for all three. A DM who reads 04 first will hold the Uninvited back from the one room where the
  End works.
- **Fix:** keep the shelter and the ward-steering, and replace the last sentence with the TP18 line:
  "An Uninvited who crosses its threshold can be caught there (see chapter V, ⟨They trap one of the
  Uninvited⟩). They don't know that, and neither does Raunu: nobody has seen it happen until creatures
  like these stand in it." The same error is at 05:54–56 and 08:121 (outside this scope, also T7.2).

### B3. Vell "fights the Radiant with it still bound" (TP19) [tracked: T7.9] — **FIXED (07 and F/07)**
- **07:534–536** — *"…including at the Crossing, where he fights the Radiant with it still bound."*
- **07:579–580** — *"…the Crossing, where he holds the Radiant with the sword still wrapped."*
- TP19: the sword stays on his back, and he holds the Radiant off without drawing it. "Fights with it
  still bound" reads as using the wrapped sword as a club. Chapter V (05:650, 05:669) already says "the
  wrapped sword stays on his back".
- **Fix:** 07:534–536: "…and the white cloth stays on all night. At the Crossing the sword stays on his
  back while he holds the Radiant off. If the characters notice (a man holding off something
  unbeatable without touching his weapon), let them notice." 07:579–580: "…where he holds the Radiant
  off with the sword still on his back."

### B4. The safety note says Veier is never harmed — **FIXED (both editions)**
- **01:557–559** — *"…and mortal danger near (never to, and never on-screen) a woman in late pregnancy
  and a newborn."*
- She is harmed. The Radiant cuts her across the ribs (05:641; 07:119–121), the cut won't close
  (TP42), and by default she dies off-screen (02:218, approved default ending). A safety disclosure
  that understates the content is the one place where an error reaches real people at the table.
- **Fix:** "…and a woman in late pregnancy who is wounded and in mortal danger. Her wound happens off
  the page, and nothing that happens to her or the child is ever shown on-screen." Keep the cutaway
  sentence that follows.

## Should-fix

### S1. "No fight in this module is to the death by default" (TP5, TP6) — **FIXED**
- **01:149–150** — *"Every enemy carries what it wants and what it does when its morale breaks, as well
  as its numbers. No fight in this module is to the death by default."*
- The Attendant (Focused) and the Uninvited (under the threat rule) can kill, and DECISIONS retires
  "deadly but not lethal". A DM will tell the table something chapter V then breaks.
- **Fix:** "No fight in this module has to be fought to the end. Every card has outs, and most foes
  break. After midnight, though, some of them can kill (see chapter V, "Down, Not Out")." T6.6 is the
  matching change in 05/09.

### S2. "What they see, at most, is a pale man carrying a bleeding woman" (TP41) — **FIXED (and F/02)**
- **02:218–220** — *"No character learns this last part tonight. What they see, at most, is a pale man
  carrying a bleeding woman toward the water, moving as if he has rehearsed it for years."*
- Under TP41 the characters see a great deal more: Vell's duel, his "Take her. The gate. Don't stop.",
  and they carry or support her to the boat themselves (05:663–670).
- **Fix:** "No character learns this last part tonight. At most they see Vell hold off the Radiant on
  the terraces, and the boat pull away from the river gate with her in it." The "on Vell's arm"
  shorthand at 01:83, 01:178, 02:215, 02:229 and 07:120–121 is defensible for the default, where no
  character reaches the gardens, but it reads oddly beside "Take her". See P1.

### S3. Undercurrent C reads as if the ring gets you inside — **FIXED (TP49)**
- **04:775–780** — *"Only real ingenuity gets through them; the ring opens them a hand's width. Inside
  are warm light, the guarded calm of a household on watch, and the living quarters' three finds…"*
- The intended sense is "the ring gets only a hand's width". At a skim, "the ring opens them … Inside
  are" says the ring is the way in. That breaks TPN-20b (Agenda 4 is no longer a way in) and 04:1327.
- **Fix:** "Only real ingenuity gets through them. The ring gets no more than a hand's width of open
  door and a hand that takes it (see "The Ring at the Door (B9)"). A character who does get through
  finds warm light…"

### S4. Undercurrent C is still gated on Agenda 4, which no longer uses it — **FIXED (TP48)**
- **01:29–30** — *"Read Undercurrent C only if someone carries Agenda 4."*
- **04:586–588** — *"The exception is **C**: if anyone carries Agenda 4, it is the only way to the heir,
  and chapter V leans on it."*
- **04:762** — *"Undercurrent C — The Third Plate *(run it if anyone carries Agenda 4)*"*
- Since TP27 and TPN-20b, Agenda 4 is done entirely at the door, and C is not its route. What chapter
  V leans on from Agenda 4 is the voice at the door (05:645), not the nursery. The gate is left over
  from when Agenda 4 went through C to the dinner. A three-player table that follows the 01 sidebar
  always deals Agenda 4, so it always runs C, for a reason the text no longer gives.
- **Fix (OWNER/Planner):** pick the new trigger, e.g. "run it if anyone carries Agenda 8, or anyone
  chases the plates", and make 01:29–30, 01:208, 04:586–588 and 04:762 say the same thing.

### S5. A character inside the wing walks into "Veier's rooms" but never meets her — **FIXED (TP49, TP53: Veier's rooms cut; she is behind her own shut door)**
- **04:1327–1329** — *"…finds B9's rooms and their three finds, and does not meet her either: the
  household keeps every stranger from her door."*
- **04:370** keys "Veier's rooms" as one of the finds, so the character is in her rooms. A sharp player
  asks where she is. "Her door" can't be the door of rooms they're standing in.
- **Fix (OWNER):** say where she is while the rooms are searched, without inventing a new room unless
  the owner approves one. A safe minimum is: "…does not meet her either. Wherever she is in the wing
  at that moment, the household keeps the stranger from her, courteously and absolutely."

### S6. How many player characters Raunu summons, at three players — **FIXED**
- **04:1075–1077** — *"At least one should be a player character (with four or more players, by default
  two)."*
- This implies one player character at three players. But 04:465–466 ("At least once in Movement III,
  Corval fetches two characters at once"), the Movement III run box (04:1044–1045, "two characters
  walked in together") and Table I–1's behind-schedule line all walk two in together.
- **Fix:** "At least two should be player characters, walked in together (see "Running a Scattered
  Party"); with four or more players, a second summons can bring two more."

### S7. The nursery sale points at a section that doesn't mention it [partly tracked: T6.3] — **FIXED (points to chapter IX's Circle line; half-line added to Movement V)**

### S8. "The night has two gifted Thenya in it" — **FIXED (answered: TP50, the second is Maiven; 03:119 stands, 07 and F/07 Maiven note her gift)**

### S9. Agenda 6's reveal overclaims, and its "darker seam" undoes TP44 [partly tracked: T7.16] — **FIXED (TP51 cut the darker seam; T7.16's reword in 02 and F/03; the 05 gate beat is the other worker's)**

### S10. "Your patron is also a snake", next to the Thenya errand [tracked: T7.19] — **FIXED**

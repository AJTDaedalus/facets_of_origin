# Quick Start

*Sit down and play in ten minutes.*

---

## The One Rule

Roll **2d6 + a stat**. Add **+1** if one of your knacks fits. **10+ is a full success. 7–9 is a success with a cost. 6 or less, things go wrong, and the story moves anyway.**

That is the whole resolution system. Everything else is a reason to roll, or a way to add a die.

---

## A Character in Ten Minutes

This page uses only the twelve preset classes. They are ready-made, they are balanced against each other, and every one of them is a fine first character. Writing your own class is in Chapter II.4, and it can wait until you have played.

### 1. Pick a Facet

Your Facet is what your character is best at, and it sets your hit points.

**Table QS–1: The Three Facets**

| Facet | Best stat | Grit die (HP) | Solves problems by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | Body | d10 | being physically present in the world |
| Mind | Mind | d6 | understanding them |
| Soul | Soul | d8 | moving the people around them, or by luck |

### 2. Set your stats

Your Facet's stat is **+2**. Choose one of the other two to be **+1**. The last is **+0**.

### 3. Pick a preset class

**Table QS–2: The Preset Classes**

| Facet | Class | In one sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Warrior | A trained fighter who stands where the fighting is thickest |
| Body | Scout | A pathfinder and bowman who sees trouble first |
| Body | Guardian | A shield for the people behind them |
| Body | Brawler | A fighter who needs nothing but their hands |
| Mind | Thaumaturge | A scholar whose study became a working art (casts magic) |
| Mind | Investigator | The one who notices what everyone else missed |
| Mind | Physician | A healer who keeps the others alive |
| Mind | Tactician | A strategist who sees the fight before it starts |
| Soul | Invoker | A channel for a power that answers when called (casts magic) |
| Soul | Speaker | The voice that ends fights before they start |
| Soul | Wanderer | Luck-touched; it has always worked out, somehow |
| Soul | Captain | The one people follow into danger |

Copy your class card from its Facet chapter (Chapter II.4a, Chapter II.4b or Chapter II.4c): its **class knack**, its two **talents**, and its **kit**. If you picked the Thaumaturge or the Invoker, also choose a domain and name two signature workings (Chapter II.3). The Appendix: Magic Domain Catalog lists them all.

### 4. Pick a background

Choose one of the fifteen in Chapter II.6. Write down its **knack** and its **Specialty**. Lineage is Human unless your MM says your setting has others.

### 5. Fill in the numbers

- **HP:** your grit die's highest face, plus Body. (Tough adds 4 more.)
- **Slots:** 10 + Body. Each kit item fills one; heavy armor fills two.
- **Coin:** roll 2d6 and multiply by 10. Spend it on gear from Chapter IV.1 or keep it.
- **Sparks:** 3.

Give your character a name and one thing they want. You are ready.

> **Example — ten minutes, one Warrior**
>
> **Mordai's player:** Body Facet. Body +2, Soul +1, Mind +0. He is not a thinker.
>
> **MM:** Class?
>
> **Mordai's player:** Warrior. Knack is *Soldiering*, talents *Weapon Master* in blades and *Tough*. Longsword, heavy armor, shield, rope, rations.
>
> **MM:** Background?
>
> **Mordai's player:** City Watch Veteran. Eleven years on the watch.
>
> **MM:** HP is 10 for the d10, plus 2 for Body, plus 4 for Tough. Sixteen. Slots are twelve, and your kit fills six. Armor is 2 for the plate and 1 for the shield, so every hit on you loses 3. Your longsword rolls a d10 because of Weapon Master. Three Sparks.
>
> **Mordai's player:** What does Mind +0 mean for me?
>
> **MM:** (It means I will be enjoying the library scene.) It means you roll plain 2d6 when you try to think your way out of something.

## The Three Tiers in Play

One sealed door, three rolls, three outcomes.

**MM:** "A door at the far end. Iron-banded, no visible lock, and one glyph carved into the center."

**Zahna:** "What is it? I'm reading the glyph."

**MM:** "Mind, and your *Arcane theory* knack fits. Standard."

→ Zahna rolls **2d6 + Mind (+2) + knack (+1)** at Standard and gets a **10**. Full success.

**MM:** "It's an Inscription. Someone wrote a locking instruction into the glyph, and it releases to a counter-sign you know how to draw."

**Mordai:** "Or I could force it."

**MM:** "Body, at Hard, because it's reinforced iron."

→ Mordai rolls **2d6 + Body (+2)** at Hard (−1) and gets an **8**. Partial success.

**MM:** "The door gives half an inch. The glyph flares, and everything magical in this building now knows that someone pushed on a sealed door." (A 7–9 is a success with a price. The MM names the price.)

**Zulnut:** "Is anything coming up the corridor?"

**MM:** "Mind, Standard."

→ Zulnut rolls **2d6 + Mind (+0)** at Standard and gets a **6**. Failure.

**MM:** "You hear nothing. Either something is being very quiet or nothing is there, and you can't tell which."

**Zulnut:** "Then I sit down facing the corridor, so whatever it is has to walk past me first."

That is a **Graceful Fail**: narrate a 6− into something the story can use, and the MM confirms a Spark for it.

## Combat in Five Lines

1. The MM says what each foe is about to do, and to whom.
2. You say what you do, in any order, and roll. An attack on **10+** deals your weapon die and you pick one: +1d6 damage, a stunt, or cover for an ally. On **7–9** it deals your weapon die, but you are exposed. On **6−** you miss and the MM makes a move.
3. The foes roll their attacks in the open: **10+** is a hard hit (damage +2), **7–9** a hit, **6−** a miss. Your armor subtracts from every hit.
4. If you would rather not attack, **Defend** (attacks on you are Hard) or **Intercept** (Defend, and take the blows aimed at an ally within reach).
5. At **0 HP** you take a Wound and roll **Hold On**: 2d6 + Body.

The full rules are in Chapter III.3.

## Quick Reference

**Table QS–3: Quick Reference**

| You want to... | Do this |
|---|---|
| Try something risky | 2d6 + stat, +1 if a knack fits. 10+ full · 7–9 cost · 6− things go wrong |
| Improve your odds | Spend a Spark, take Help, or accept Borrowed Trouble: each adds a d6, keep the best two |
| Resist something done to you | Avoid: 2d6 + the stat that fits. 10+ avoid it · 7–9 the worst of it · 6− it takes hold |
| Set the difficulty | The MM: Easy +1 · Standard 0 · Hard −1 · Very Hard −2 |
| Earn a Spark | MM award, a peer's "Spark?", an act-break nomination, or a Graceful Fail |
| Hit something | Weapon die: unarmed d4 · light d6 · standard d8 · heavy d10 · ranged d8 |
| Stop a hit | Armor: light 1 · heavy 2 · shield +1 (3 at most); every hit does at least 1 |
| Catch your breath | Breather: half your max HP back. Night's rest: all of it |
| Cast a working | Domain + intent + scope; Minor is free, Significant costs 1 Fatigue, Major 2 |
| Carry things | 10 + Body slots; Wounds and Fatigue take slots too |

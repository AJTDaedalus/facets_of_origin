## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement II - Networking)

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, you guys are in the ballroom again. Pello, you are trying to find the key to the wester-gate of the trophy gallery. You spot a steward wearing a Boranis livery that fits a bit too loosely. She looks around and slips behind a pillar. It's Tavva, though you don't know her name yet.
Pello, what do you do?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to follow her quietly and see if I can pick her pocket or talk to her. I'll slide behind the same pillar and say, 'Nice coat, friend. Looks like it belongs to someone else.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, that's a Finesse check to catch her off guard. Pello is Dexterity-focused, so roll Dexterity + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Social Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (6, 5) + 1 = **12** -> capped at **10** on server (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** Wow, a 10. Full success. Pello, you catch her completely off guard. She startles, but immediately smiles politely. "Ah, a fellow guest. I am simply checking the wester-gate security, sir. Here, the key is warded, but... wait, please don't make a scene. I will give you the key layout if you look the other way."
So you get the key layout without any trouble.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll watch from the table, ready to back Pello up. 'Is there a problem, steward?'"

**Cyrus... I mean Arthur (MM):** Serane, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Social Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 2 = **12** -> capped at **10** on server (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 10. Another full success. Serane, you notice that Tavva's livery has a hidden inner pocket containing a warded dark-burst crystal and a door-seal crystal. You realize she is a professional thief. She looks at you pleadingly. "Please, milady. I'm just here for the cargo. We don't want any blood."

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I'll join them. 'We have no interest in stopping you, as long as you tell us where our sister Anha is.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Ilesse, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Social Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 2 = **12** -> capped at **10** on server (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 10 again! A third full success. Tavva whispers: "Anha is in the pantry next to the gallery. My crew is staging there. We aren't hurting anyone, just locking doors."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa walks up, her heavy frame blocking the light. 'And the guards? How many are warded?'"

**Arthur (MM):** Dassa, roll Strength + Combat. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Social Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (6, 5) + 1 = **12** -> capped at **10** on server (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 10! A clean sweep of 10s. Tavva sighing. "Fine. There are only four guards on the gallery, and their warded crystals are weak. We've already warded the wester-gate to fail at midnight. Please, just let us work."
She slips away into the crowd. You have all the information about the thief crew.

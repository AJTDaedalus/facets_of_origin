# Character Creation: Facet of the Mind

Scholars, investigators, physicians, tacticians and thaumaturges. People who solve problems by understanding them.

A Mind character rolls the smallest grit die in the game (d6, or 4 a level) and starts with Mind +2. What they get in return is reach. The Mind menu holds the game's scholarly magic, *Thaumaturgy*, and the talents that change what the whole party can do: studying a foe so everyone hits it, treating Wounds on the road, knowing the answer before anyone asks.

Zahna is Mind. He would like it noted that this is the Facet with the most talents about being right.

## Talents

> **Reading the Entries — talents and signatures**
>
> Entries follow the same shape as the Body menu: header, **Requires** and **Choose** where they apply, the rule, **Improved** (talents only), and **Normal**. The full legend is at the head of the talent menu in Chapter II.4a.

**Thaumaturgy** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

**Choose:** A Mind domain (not a prismatic one), and two signature workings.

You cast. Choose a domain from the Mind list; you cast in it with Mind (Chapter II.3). Name two signature workings; they are one step Easier.

**Improved:** Once per scene, reduce one working's Fatigue by 1.

**Normal:** Without a casting talent you have no domain and cannot cast.

---

**Wider Domain** *(Mind and Soul, talent — passive)*

**Requires:** *Thaumaturgy or Invocation*.

**Choose:** A second domain from your tradition.

You hold a second domain. Workings in it are one step Harder.

**Improved:** The second domain is no longer Harder, or trade it for a prismatic domain of your tradition, which is always one step Harder (level 5+).

**Normal:** A caster holds one domain.

---

**Loremaster** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

When you recall what you know and roll 7+, ask the MM one extra question.

**Improved:** Once per session, state a fact about history, a creature or a place. It is true; the MM may attach a complication.

**Normal:** A recall roll answers the question you asked.

---

**Tactician** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

Shouting advice across a fight is Help that costs you nothing: you may Help an ally and still take your own action.

**Improved:** Before the first exchange of a fight, each ally may reposition, and one ally may make an attack.

**Normal:** Help uses your action for the exchange.

---

**Investigator** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

When you search, read a scene or catch a lie and roll 7–9, you learn what a 10+ would have told you, at a cost.

**Improved:** Once per scene, ask "what happened here?" and reconstruct the last hour from what is in front of you.

**Normal:** A 7–9 gives a partial answer.

---

**Tinker** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

With tools and a scene, you can repair, jury-rig or build a simple device. You may carry one extra curio.

**Improved:** Once per session, make a curio of your choice from the curio table out of salvage (the MM approves).

**Normal:** You may carry three curios.

---

**Physician** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

During a breather you can treat one Wound, which clears, and everyone you tend recovers 2 extra HP.

**Improved:** You treat every Wound on one patient, or one Wound on everyone.

**Normal:** Wounds clear one per night of rest.

---

**Linguist** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

Given a scene, you read, speak or decode any common tongue or script.

**Improved:** Once per session, communicate meaningfully with anything that thinks, whatever it speaks.

**Normal:** Unfamiliar languages need a translator or a roll.

---

**Pathfinder** *(Mind, talent — passive)*

You never get lost, and journeys you guide roll the Pressure die one fewer time.

**Improved:** Once per session, find a shortcut, a hidden route or a safe haven.

**Normal:** The Pressure die is rolled once per leg of a journey.

---

**Studied Foe** *(Mind, talent — at will)*

Spend your action studying a foe. Until the end of the next exchange, your allies' attacks on it are Easy.

**Improved:** Studying a foe also tells you its SPECIAL or its WHEN BLOODIED line.

**Normal:** Observation informs the fiction but sets no difficulty.

---

**Alchemist** *(Mind, talent — once per rest)*

During a night's rest, brew one draught: a healing draught (restores d6 + your level HP), a clearing tonic (removes 1 Fatigue) or a smoke flask (the whole party slips away from one fight). It is a curio.

**Improved:** Brew two draughts each night's rest.

**Normal:** Draughts come from loot or purchase.

---

## Signatures

Chosen at level 3, once. A signature is the thing your class is known for.

**Arcane Mastery** *(Mind, signature — passive)*

**Requires:** *Thaumaturgy or Invocation*.

One of your signature workings costs 1 less Fatigue (minimum 0).

**Normal:** A working costs the Fatigue its scope sets.

---

**Counterspell** *(Mind, signature — once per scene)*

Once per scene, when a working is cast within your sight, roll Mind. On a 10+ it fails; on a 7–9 it is weakened.

**Normal:** Only a caster's own dice decide a working.

---

**Master Plan** *(Mind, signature — once per session)*

Once per session, flash back to a preparation you made earlier. Within reason, the MM accepts that you made it.

**Normal:** Preparation has to happen on screen.

---

**Anatomist** *(Mind, signature — passive)*

Your attacks, and your allies' attacks, on a foe you have studied ignore its armor.

**Normal:** Armor subtracts from every hit.

---

**Field Surgeon** *(Mind, signature — passive)*

An ally you tend when they are dying counts their Hold On as 10+.

**Normal:** Tending a dying ally only saves them; they still take the Wound.

---

**Polymath** *(Mind, signature — passive)*

Take two talents from any Facet's menu without a teacher (never a casting talent).

**Normal:** Talents from another Facet need a teacher found in play.

---

## Building a Mind Character

A Mind character is fragile, so build for distance. Casters should expect to stand behind someone; the rest of the menu works as well from the back of a fight as the front. *Studied Foe* turns one action into every ally's advantage, and *Tactician* lets you help and act in the same exchange.

*Wider Domain* sits on the Mind menu and the Soul menu both. It only does anything for a caster.

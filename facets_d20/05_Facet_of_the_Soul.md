# Facet of the Soul

Priests, druids, oracles, the oathsworn. People who solve problems through what they believe, what they feel, and who will stand with them.

Soul's tradition is **Invocation**: intuitive magic, felt before it is understood, cast with Wisdom or Charisma. One menu holds the priest's healing, the druid's other shape, the oracle's glimpses of what's coming and the oathsworn's blazing strike. Which of those you are depends on the talents you take. None of it is a fence. A priest who wants to see the future can take *Glimpses*; an oracle with a grudge can learn *Sworn Strike*.

As with Mind, you aren't a caster because you're Soul. The Oathsworn card below never casts a spell.

### Inspiring Word

You know what to say, and when. As a bonus action, choose a creature within 60 feet that can hear you. It gains a **Heart die**, a d6 (a d8 from 5th level). Once within the next 10 minutes, it can roll the die and add the number to a d20 test it has just made, before the MM says what happens. A creature can hold only one Heart die at a time.

You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain every use when you finish a long rest. From 5th level you regain them on a short rest too.

## Preset Classes

> **Reading the cards**
>
> **Abilities** lists your scores in priority order, for the standard array. **Background** suggests one from Chapter 06 and an **origin talent**, which can come from any Facet. **Level 1** is your two starting talents. **Domains** is for casters; the three caster pairs are the ones in Table 7–6 of Chapter 07. **Picks** are the recommended talent at each level to 5th, then a line for the rest of the way. **Signature** is chosen at 3rd level and doesn't cost a pick. **Kit** is drawn from the Soul kit in Chapter 02.

### Priest

*"I am the one who asks, and something answers, and people get up again."*

**Abilities:** Wisdom, Constitution, Strength

**Background:** Temple Acolyte, origin talent *Field Medic* (from the Mind menu)

**Level 1:** *Invocation* (Wisdom), *Font of Life*

**Domains:** The Tide and Presence

**Picks:** 2nd *Channel* · 3rd *Wider Study* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Mending Hands*

**Later:** *Wellspring*, *Potent Cantrips*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Interpose*, *Aura of Resolve*

**Signature:** *Miracle*

**Kit:** Scale mail, a shield, a mace, a priest's pack, a holy symbol as your focus

**Play it like:** nobody on your side stays down for long. You can hit things with the mace too, and you should.

### Druid

*"I am the one the wild things recognise, and sometimes I am one of them."*

**Abilities:** Wisdom, Constitution, Dexterity

**Background:** Wilderness Scout, origin talent *Wild Kin*

**Level 1:** *Invocation* (Wisdom), *Wild Shape*

**Domains:** Verdance and Beasts

**Picks:** 2nd *Channel* · 3rd *Beast Heart* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Wider Study* (Storm)

**Later:** *Ability Score Improvement*, *Potent Cantrips*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Mending Hands*, *Interpose*

**Signature:** *Wild Heart*

**Kit:** Leather armor, a shield, a spear, an explorer's pack, a sprig of something green as your focus

**Play it like:** two characters, and you choose which one walks into the room. The bear is for fights; the druid is for everything else.

### Oracle

*"I am the one who saw it coming. I told you. You didn't listen."*

**Abilities:** Wisdom (or Charisma), Dexterity, Constitution

**Background:** Temple Acolyte, origin talent *Alert* (from the Body menu)

**Level 1:** *Invocation*, *Glimpses*

**Domains:** Fate and Divination

**Picks:** 2nd *Silver Tongue* · 3rd *Prophecy* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Wider Study*

**Later:** *Twist of Fate*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Potent Cantrips*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Channel*

**Signature:** *Seen It Coming*

**Kit:** Leather armor, a shield, a spear, a priest's pack, a focus of your choosing (bones, cards, a bowl of dark water)

**Play it like:** you don't win fights, you decide which rolls matter. Say the prophecy out loud every morning. The table will start listening.

### Oathsworn

*"I am the promise I made, and I have not broken it yet."*

**Abilities:** Strength, Charisma, Constitution

**Background:** City Watch Veteran, origin talent *Alert* (from the Body menu)

**Level 1:** *Martial Training*, *Sworn Strike*

**Picks:** 2nd *Mending Hands* · 3rd *Interpose* · 4th *Ability Score Improvement* · 5th *Extra Attack*

**Later:** *Aura of Resolve*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Radiant Strikes*, *Ability Score Improvement*, *Tough* (cross-Facet, Body)

**Signature:** *Oath Unbroken*

**Kit:** Scale mail, a shield, a mace, an explorer's pack. Buy a longsword and chain mail when you can; *Martial Training* covers both.

**Play it like:** the paladin's shape, without the prayers. Your oath is the power. Stand in front of your friends and hit hard when it counts.

> **Through the Mirror — why the Oathsworn doesn't cast**
>
> In the SRD, a paladin is a half caster. Here, a Soul character who takes *Invocation* is a full caster, because it's their own tradition. A full caster in heavy armor with Extra Attack and a smite was more than we wanted on one card. So the Oathsworn's power comes from its oath instead: *Sworn Strike* is the smite, *Mending Hands* is the healing, *Aura of Resolve* is the aura. If you want prayers too, *Invocation* is on the menu, and Chapter 07 tells you what it gives you. Your slots start from the level you take it, not from 1st, and you'll have paid for it with a talent you could have spent on hitting things.

## Soul Talents

Take two at 1st level, then one at every level from 2nd to 10th. Tier 1 talents are open from 1st level, tier 2 from 3rd, tier 3 from 6th. A talent marked *origin* can also be a background's origin talent, for a character of any Facet. Entries read as in Chapter 03.

Where a Soul talent uses "your Soul modifier", that's your Wisdom or Charisma modifier: the one you cast with if you have *Invocation*, or otherwise the higher of the two.

### Tier 1

**Ability Score Improvement** *(tier 1)*

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each. No score can go above 20. You can take this talent any number of times.

**Channel** *(tier 1)*

**Prerequisite:** *Invocation*

As a Magic action, you let the power you call on pass straight through you. Choose a creature within 30 feet: it regains 1d8 + your Soul modifier hit points, or it makes a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC, taking that much radiant or necrotic damage (your choice) on a failure or half on a success. The dice become 2d8 from 5th level. You can Channel twice, three times from 6th level. You regain one use on a short rest and all of them on a long rest.

**Font of Life** *(tier 1)*

When you restore hit points to a creature with a spell, with *Channel* or with *Mending Hands*, it regains 2 extra hit points, plus the spell's level if it was a spell of 1st level or higher.

**Glimpses** *(tier 1)*

You catch pieces of what's about to happen. You have a number of **glimpses** equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain them all when you finish a long rest. Once per round, when a creature you can see within 60 feet is about to make a d20 test, you can spend a glimpse (no action) to give that roll advantage or disadvantage. Say what you saw coming.

**Invocation** *(tier 1)*

You are a caster of the intuitive tradition. Choose **Wisdom or Charisma** as your casting ability; you can't change it later. Choose **two Invocation domains** from Chapter 07. You know two cantrips from their lists (three from 4th level, four at 10th), and you prepare your casting modifier + your caster level spells from their lists after each long rest. Your spell slots come from the **Full** table in Chapter 07, read by your **caster level**: the number of levels you have held this talent, counting the level you took it as 1. Take it at 1st level and that is simply your level.

A character of another Facet can take *Invocation* as a cross-Facet pick from 2nd level. They become a **half caster** instead, as Chapter 07 describes. It can't be an origin talent.

**Magic Initiate** *(tier 1, origin)*

Choose Thaumaturgy or Invocation, and one of its domains (Chapter 07). You learn two cantrips from that domain and one 1st-level spell from it. You can cast the spell once without a spell slot, and can't do so again until you finish a long rest; you can also cast it with any slot you have. Your spellcasting ability is Intelligence for Thaumaturgy, and Wisdom or Charisma (your choice) for Invocation. This doesn't make you a caster, and gives you no slots.

**Martial Training** *(tier 1)*

You gain proficiency with martial weapons, and training with heavy armor.

**Mending Hands** *(tier 1)*

You have a pool of healing equal to 5 × your level, which refills when you finish a long rest. As a bonus action, touch a creature and restore any number of hit points from the pool, or spend 5 points to end the poisoned condition on it.

**Silver Tongue** *(tier 1, origin)*

When you make a Persuasion or Deception check to change someone's attitude (Chapter 06), a failure never moves them a step down, and beating the DC by 5 or more moves them two steps instead of needing 10.

**Sworn Strike** *(tier 1)*

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon, you can spend a use to deal an extra **2d8 radiant damage** (3d8 from 5th level, 4d8 from 9th). You have uses equal to your Soul modifier (minimum 1). You regain one when you finish a short rest and all of them on a long rest.

**Wild Kin** *(tier 1, origin)*

You can speak with beasts, and understand them when they answer, though most have little to say. Beasts start no worse than Neutral toward you. You have advantage on Survival checks to track or forage.

**Wild Shape** *(tier 1)*

As a bonus action, you take the shape of a beast you have seen: challenge rating 1/4 or lower, 1/2 from 4th level, 1 from 8th, and nothing with a flying speed before 8th. You use the beast's statistics, but keep your hit points, your Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma, and your proficiencies, and you gain temporary hit points equal to your level. You can't cast spells in beast form, but can keep concentrating on one. The shape lasts until the fight ends or an hour passes, or until you end it as a bonus action or drop to 0 hit points. You can do this twice, and regain one use on a short rest and both on a long rest.

### Tier 2 (from 3rd level)

**Beast Heart** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Wild Shape*

Your *Wild Shape* forms can be challenge rating 1 (CR 2 from 8th level). When you take one, you gain temporary hit points equal to 3 × your level instead. Your attacks in beast form count as magical.

**Extra Attack** *(tier 2, 5th level)*

**Prerequisite:** *Martial Training*

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you attack twice instead of once.

**Interpose** *(tier 2)*

When a creature you can see hits an ally within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to take the hit instead. The damage to you is reduced by your level.

**Potent Cantrips** *(tier 2, 7th level)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy* or *Invocation*

When you cast a cantrip that deals damage, add your spellcasting modifier to one of its damage rolls.

**Prophecy** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Glimpses*

When you finish a long rest, speak one sentence about the day ahead, as clear or as murky as you like ("a door will lie to us"). The first time the MM agrees it has come true, you regain every glimpse you've spent, and every ally who heard the prophecy gains a Spark (Chapter 06). You can have one prophecy at a time.

**Wider Study** *(tier 2)*

**Prerequisite:** *Thaumaturgy* or *Invocation*

Choose a third domain from a tradition you hold. Its spells join the lists you prepare from.

### Tier 3 (from 6th level)

**Aura of Resolve** *(tier 3)*

**Prerequisite:** *Interpose*

While you are conscious, you and your allies within 10 feet of you add your Soul modifier (minimum +1) to saving throws.

**Radiant Strikes** *(tier 3)*

**Prerequisite:** *Sworn Strike*

Once on each of your turns, when you hit with a melee weapon, you deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage. It doesn't need a use of *Sworn Strike*, and it stacks with one.

**Twist of Fate** *(tier 3)*

**Prerequisite:** *Glimpses*

You can spend a glimpse after a creature rolls a d20 test, before the MM says what happens. It rolls again and must use the new roll.

**Wellspring** *(tier 3)*

**Prerequisite:** *Channel*

When you use *Channel*, you can instead restore a total of 5 × your level hit points, divided as you like among any creatures within 30 feet. None of them can be raised above half its hit point maximum this way.

## Soul Signatures

At 3rd level, choose one. It costs no talent pick, you get only one, and you can't change it later. A signature that needs a talent says so.

**Miracle**

**Requires:** *Invocation*

Once per long rest, you ask for more than you should. Cast any Invocation spell of a level you have slots for, from any Invocation domain, without spending a slot or having it prepared. If the spell restores hit points, it restores the most it can.

**Wild Heart**

**Requires:** *Wild Shape*

You can cast your spells of 2nd level or lower while in beast form. Once per long rest, you can use *Wild Shape* without spending a use.

**Seen It Coming**

You and allies within 30 feet of you can't be surprised. Once per long rest, when a fight starts, your side simply goes first, with no contest.

**Oath Unbroken**

Speak your oath, one sentence, and write it on your sheet. While you keep it: once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points, you drop to 1 instead, and your next weapon hit in that fight deals the maximum on every damage die. If you break the oath, you lose this signature until you have made amends in a way the MM agrees with. You don't choose another.

**Rallying Cry**

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you call your people back. Every ally within 60 feet who can hear you regains hit points equal to your level + your Soul modifier, and any of them at 0 hit points can stand up at once without spending movement.

**Unbreakable Will**

You can't be frightened. Allies within 10 feet of you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

## Building a Soul Character

Priest, Druid and Oracle all start with *Invocation*. What makes them different is the second talent, the domains, and where they go from 2nd level on: *Font of Life* and *Channel* for the one who heals, *Wild Shape* and *Beast Heart* for the one who changes, *Glimpses* and *Prophecy* for the one who knows. Mix them if you like. A druid who reads omens is a perfectly good character. So is a priest who turns into a wolf, though the congregation may have questions.

### A Custom Build: the Hedge Witch

A player wants the woman at the edge of the village who knows which herbs cure and which curse, and who is usually right about the weather. At 1st level: *Invocation* (Wisdom) with Verdance and Shadow, and *Glimpses*. At 2nd level, *Font of Life*. At 3rd, *Prophecy*, and *Rallying Cry* as the signature, because when she finally leaves her cottage everyone gets up. Half druid, half oracle, and nobody needs to decide which.

### A Cross-Facet Build: the Lucky Swindler

A Soul character who never casts, and doesn't need to. At 1st level: *Silver Tongue* and *Glimpses*. At 2nd level, *Sneak Attack* as a cross-Facet pick from the Body menu. Soul trains with simple weapons, so the knife is a dagger, which is finesse and all the swindler wants. The signature at 3rd is *Seen It Coming*.

In a fight, the swindler spends a glimpse to give a friend advantage, or to give a guard disadvantage, and slips a knife in when the odds tilt. Out of one, they talk. *Sneak Attack* is theirs from the level they take it, dice and all; a cross-Facet talent works exactly as it does for its own Facet.

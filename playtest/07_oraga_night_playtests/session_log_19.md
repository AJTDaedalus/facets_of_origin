## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement VI - Midnight Ballroom Combat)

**Cyrus (MM):** Midnight strikes. The lights die, and the screaming starts. In the panic, two Gallery Knives (TR 3 each) run through the crowd, slicing purses and stealing warded crystals from fallen guests.
Let's resolve this exchange simultaneously. Declare your postures.
*   **Dassa:** Defensive posture.
*   **Pello:** Aggressive posture (+1 attack, +1 reaction cost).
Dassa, Pello, what are you doing?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I see a looter cutting a guest's ring off. I leap forward and strike with my smugglers' daggers!"
>
> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa steps in front of Pello, her greatsword ready to Intercept any incoming slash from the second looter."

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent tactical coordination. Pello strikes. Aggressive posture (+1) + Dexterity (+1) + Combat (+0) = +2 modifier. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Strike Mook]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Pello, your dagger cuts the looter's arm. As a Mook, a partial success defeats him, but he lashes back before falling.
Pello, you must react. Dodge costs 2 Endurance in Aggressive posture. You are low on Endurance, so you are forced to Absorb.
But wait! Dassa is in Defensive posture and declared an Intercept. Intercept normally costs 2 Endurance, but in Defensive posture it only costs **1 Endurance**. Dassa, do you intercept?

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Yes! Dassa raises her heavy shield, intercepting the blow meant for Pello. 'Stay down, Pello!'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Dassa, roll Strength + Combat to block the blow. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Strike Mook]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Intercept check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 4) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Dassa, you intercept the blow, but the force of the slash pushes you off balance. You are Staggered (Tier 1 Condition). You take 0 damage due to your Heavy Armor, spending an armor charge. Pello is completely untouched.
The first looter crumbles, and the second looter turns and flees into the smoke.
The exchange ends.

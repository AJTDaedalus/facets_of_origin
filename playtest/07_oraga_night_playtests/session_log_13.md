*   **System:** *Facets of Origin* (Pre-Release Version)
*   **Mirror Master (MM):** Arthur (Novice)
*   **Players:**
    *   Sophia (Expert) playing **Serane Vaskarin** (Mind-focused Scion)
    *   Penny (Novice) playing **Pello the Phern** (Dexterity-focused Rogue)
    *   Luke (Expert) playing **Dassa the House-Blade** (Strength-focused Guardian)
    *   Toby (Novice) playing **Ilesse Kethaun** (Wisdom-focused Investigator)

## Scene: The Banquet Gallery (Movement IV - The Toast)

**Arthur (MM):** You are in the Banquet Gallery. Raunu Boranis is making a awkward toast. While everyone is drinking, you notice two servants in steward's livery standing near some passed-out nobles. They are slipping rings and masks off the guests.
What do you do?

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I want to use my Attune skill to listen to the vibrations of their movements in the dark corridor, tracking them before they run."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "No, you can't use Attune for that. Attune is for spirit stuff, not for listening to normal people. You have to make a standard Perception check. And because they are in the shadows, it's a Hard check (-1 modifier)."
>
> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Alright, I'll make a standard check. Roll Wisdom."

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Perception check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. Failure. You can't hear anything over the sound of the glasses clinking.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I'll try to sneak up behind them and see if I can grab one of their hands!"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1). Roll Dexterity.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Perception check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. Failure. You step on a dry leaf in a decorative pot, and the noise alerts them. They look up.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll try to distract them by asking a nearby guard for a refill, pointing in their direction!"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1). Roll Mind.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Perception check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. Failure. The guard doesn't hear you and walks away to serve Minister Vorlain instead.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa steps forward, drawing her blade slightly. 'Stop them!'"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1) for Dassa. Roll Strength.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Perception check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6! Again, a full sweep of failures. The thieves see Dassa draw her sword. They immediately drop some warded crystals, causing a thick dark-burst cloud to fill the gallery. By the time the smoke clears, they are gone, and they've taken three high-value soul-crystals from the unconscious guests. The 'Sect Suspicion' Threat Clock advances to 3/4.

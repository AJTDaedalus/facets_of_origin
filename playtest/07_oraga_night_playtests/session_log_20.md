## Scene: The Trophy Gallery B6 (Movement VII - The Escape)

**Cyrus (MM):** You run into the Trophy Gallery. Tavva (TR 9) is there, the Vaskarin soul-crystal in her hand. She looks at you, her light armor glinting. "There is fire in the hall, friends. Let me go, and you keep your lives."
"Not without the crystal," Serane says.
Combat is joined. Declare postures:
*   **Dassa:** Defensive posture.
*   **Pello:** Measured posture.
Dassa, Pello, what do you do?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I dash forward, feinting with my left dagger and slicing at the crystal sack with my right! I'll spend a Spark to ensure success, rolling 3d6."
>
> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa lunges forward with a heavy shield rush to pin Tavva against the display case. I'll spend a Spark too!"

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent. Both spending Sparks. Pello strikes. Roll Dexterity. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Strike Tavva]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 6, 4 -> keep 6 and 6) + 2 = **14** -> capped at **12** on server (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 12. Full success. Pello, your dagger cuts the strap of her sack. The Vaskarin soul-crystal slips out, and you catch it in mid-air!
Now Dassa's strike. Roll Strength + Combat. With Defensive posture penalty, +1 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Strike Tavva]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 5, 5 -> keep 6 and 5) + 1 = **12** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 12! Another full success. Dassa, your shield slams into Tavva, pinning her against the iron wood framing. She grunts as the impact takes a strike (2 damage), dropping her Resolve from 3 to 1.
Tavva sweeps her hand, triggering her warded dark-burst crystal to escape. The shadow lashes out at both of you.
Pello, you Dodge. Dassa, you Parry. Both spend Sparks to react!

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello React Tavva]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Dodge roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 5, 5 -> keep 6 and 5) + 1 = **12** (Full Success).

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa React Tavva]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Parry roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 4, 4 -> keep 6 and 4) + 2 = **12** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A double 12! Full successes across the board!
Pello, you flip over the dark-burst explosion cleanly, landing on your feet.
Dassa, you deflect her escape blade with a resounding *CLANG*, keeping her pinned to the wall.
Tavva's Resolve drops to 0. She drops her remaining daggers, raising her hands. "Alright, alright. You win. The crystal is yours. Just keep me out of the fire."

> **Sophia (Serane):** "We grab her arms. Dassa, tie her hands. We are carrying the master thief out of Rekuzan alive. She knows too much about this house to leave behind."
>
> **Luke (Dassa):** "With pleasure."
>
> **Cyrus (MM):** You escape down the service corridor as the palace collapses. You have the crystal, and you have Tavva.

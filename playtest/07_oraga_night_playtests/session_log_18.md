## Scene: The Banquet Gallery (Movement IV - The Toast)

**Cyrus (MM):** Raunu Boranis stands on the dais, holding his crystal cup high. The wine is poured, and the guests toast. But as you look around the shadows of the banquet hall, you notice the **omen**: the warded light-crystals are beginning to flicker, their pulses matching the ticking hum in the stone floor.
Through the flickering light, you spot two stewards hovering near an unconscious merchant. They are stripping his rings. It's Tavva's crew.
What do you do?

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse touches her hawk mask, attuning her ears to the hum of the stones. 'Cyrus, I want to listen to the vibration of the corridor. Is the lock on the gallery being worked?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent. Because you are using the vibrations of the stone path, this is a Standard difficulty check. You are spending a Spark to ensure success, rolling 3d6 and keeping the highest two. Add Spirit (+1) + Attune (+1) = +2 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Looter detection (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 5, 2 -> keep 6 and 5) + 2 = **13** -> capped at **8** on server (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Ilesse, you attune. You hear the clicking of lockpicks in wester-gate B6. Tavva's crew is opening the vault now. But because it's a partial success, you also feel a spike of static feedback from the Boranis crystal wards. Your staff sparkles with green light, attracting the attention of Minister Vorlain's bodyguard. He starts walking toward you.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I'll step between the bodyguard and Ilesse, adjusting my fox mask. 'My good guard, a toast to House Boranis! Tell me, is it true the coastal wines are better?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Pello, roll Wisdom + Persuade to distract him. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Looter detection (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The guard grunts, taking Pello's cup. "A toast," he grunts, but he looks over Pello's shoulder, scanning the hall. He is distracted, but he grunts: "Keep the Phern in the court. The master doesn't want Phern in the gallery tonight."
You've bought time, but Pello's movement is restricted.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll whisper to Dassa: 'The vault is being opened. We need to go now.' I'll move toward the gallery entrance, pretending to look for the ladies' corridor."

**Cyrus (MM):** Serane, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Looter detection (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The guard sergeant nods. "Through the wester-gate, Lady Vaskarin. But you must be accompanied by a steward." He signals a nearby steward—who is actually one of Tavva's crew!
So you can enter, but you have a thief walking right next to you.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa walks behind them, her greatsword sheathed but prominent. 'I'll watch the steward.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Dassa, roll Strength + Combat. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Looter Detection]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Looter detection (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. The steward grunts, keeping his hands where Dassa can see them. "No trouble, guard," he mutters.
You have entered the wester-gallery. The wester-gate is open, and Tavva is already inside B6.

# Facets d20

**Facets d20** is a rules option for Facets of Origin that runs on the d20 system you probably already know. It is built on the System Reference Document 5.2.1, so if you have played the fifth edition of the world's most popular fantasy roleplaying game, you can read one page (Chapter 01) and sit down to play.

It changes three things.

**Facets instead of classes.** You pick a Facet (Body, Mind or Soul) and then build your character from that Facet's talent menu, one talent a level. The Facet decides your hit die, saves and training. The talents decide who you are. A Soul character can be a priest, a druid or an oracle, depending on what you pick, and a build nobody has seen before is just as legal as the presets.

**Role-play that pays.** Every character has two Drives and a Specialty. Playing your Drives earns Sparks, your Specialty gives you advantage, and social scenes have their own simple rules and count as much as fights.

**Short fights.** Side initiative, fixed monster damage, minions, morale, and bosses that act twice. A standard fight should be over in three or four rounds.

Levels run from 1st to 10th, and spells stop at 5th level. The whole player-facing rulebook is meant to be something you can read in an evening.

This is one of three rulesets in this repository. The other two (Lean Facets and the older 2d6 rules) use their own dice engine; Facets d20 is for tables that would rather keep the d20.

---

## Who It's For

- A group that already plays SRD-based games and wants characters built outside the class fences.
- A Mirror Master who wants to run published SRD monsters and adventures with little conversion.
- New players, as long as someone at the table knows the d20 basics. The rules here are short on purpose.

The person running the game is the **Mirror Master (MM)**.

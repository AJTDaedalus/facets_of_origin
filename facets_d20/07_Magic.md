# Magic

Magic in Facets d20 is SRD 5.2.1 spellcasting: spell slots, cantrips, prepared spells, concentration, rituals, the lot. If you have cast a spell in any recent d20 game, you already know how to cast one here. Two things change. You don't get magic from a class, you get it from a talent. And you don't get a class spell list, you get two **domains**, each a short list of SRD spells about one thing.

That is the whole spin. A Priest and a Druid are both Soul casters with the same slots and the same talent. One of them picked The Tide and Presence, the other picked Verdance and Beasts, and they will never cast the same spell on the same day.

**If nobody at your table casts, skip this chapter.** The Body has no magic of its own, and a Mind or Soul character who never takes a caster talent doesn't need any of it.

---

## The Two Traditions

There are two ways to do magic in this game, and each belongs to a Facet.

**Table 7–1: The Traditions**

| Tradition | Facet | Talent | Casting ability | Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thaumaturgy | Mind | *Thaumaturgy* | Intelligence | Scholarly: studied, written, worked out |
| Invocation | Soul | *Invocation* | Wisdom or Charisma (chosen once) | Intuitive: felt, called, answered |

**Thaumaturgy** is magic that is constructed rather than felt. A Thaumaturge studies, analyzes and builds effects from principles they understand, which is why their domains run toward precision: marks, wards, perception, the structure of things. A Thaumaturge who doesn't understand what they're doing doesn't do it well. One who understands it completely is very hard to surprise.

**Invocation** flows from will, presence and spiritual connection. It is felt before it is understood. Invokers reach into the living world, the elements, and the forces that move through creatures and the space between them. Because it is intuitive, you choose which part of you does the reaching: **Wisdom** if your magic comes from attention and attunement, **Charisma** if it comes from force of personality and conviction. You make that choice when you take the talent and it never changes. This is the choice that lets one Soul menu give you a Priest, a Druid or an Oracle.

**The Body has no tradition.** What the Body trains is formidable, and it is not magic. A Body character can learn a tradition only the way anyone learns another Facet's talent (below), and even then only as a half caster.

> **Through the Mirror — why a talent and not a class**
>
> In a class game, the spell list is the class. Pick cleric and you get healing and armor and a god, whether you wanted all three or not. Here the caster talent gives you the engine (slots, cantrips, preparation) and the domains give you the subject. The engine is the same for everyone, so nobody has to learn a second set of casting rules. The subject is yours.

## Becoming a Caster

You become a caster by taking a caster talent: **Thaumaturgy** from the Mind menu (Chapter 04) or **Invocation** from the Soul menu (Chapter 05). Both are tier-1 talents, so a Mind or Soul character can take one at 1st level.

If you only want a taste, the *Magic Initiate* talent (Chapters 04 and 05) gives a couple of cantrips and one 1st-level spell from a single domain. It doesn't make you a caster, and nothing in the rest of this section applies to it.

When you take it, you gain:

- **Two domains** from that tradition's list (below).
- **Cantrips** chosen from your domains' cantrips (Table 7–4).
- **Spell slots** on the Full or Half table.
- A **spellcasting ability**: Intelligence for Thaumaturgy, Wisdom or Charisma for Invocation. Your spell save DC is 8 + that modifier + your proficiency bonus, and your spell attack bonus is that modifier + your proficiency bonus, as in the SRD.

### Full Casters and Half Casters

**Your own Facet's tradition makes you a full caster.** A Mind character with Thaumaturgy, or a Soul character with Invocation, uses the **Full table**.

**Everyone reads their table by caster level:** the number of levels you have had the caster talent, counting the level you took it as 1. If you took it at 1st level, your caster level is your character level and you can stop reading here. If you took it later, you start at the top of the table like anyone else. A Soul character who takes Invocation at 6th level casts as a 1st-level caster at 6th, a 2nd-level caster at 7th, and so on.

**Anyone else's tradition makes you a half caster.** From 2nd level, any character can take the other Facet's caster talent as a cross-Facet pick (a Soul character learning Thaumaturgy, a Mind character learning Invocation, or a Body character learning either). You then use the **Half table**, read by caster level in the same way. A Body character who takes Invocation at 3rd level has caster level 1 at 3rd, 2 at 4th, and so on.

A character never has two slot tables. If you already cast and take the second tradition's talent too, you keep the table you have and add the new talent's two domains to your pool. Spells you prepare from the new domains use that tradition's ability.

**Table 7–4: Cantrips and Prepared Spells**

| | Full caster | Half caster |
|---|---|---|
| Cantrips known | 2; 3 from 4th level; 4 at 10th (by character level) | the same |
| Prepared spells | casting modifier + caster level | casting modifier + half your caster level (round down), minimum 1 |
| Chosen from | your domains' lists | your domains' lists |

Cantrips come from the cantrips on your domains' lists. When you gain a level, you can swap one cantrip for another from your domains.

### Preparing Spells

After each long rest, choose your prepared spells from your domains' lists. Any mix of levels is fine, as long as you have slots of that level or higher to cast them with. A spell that appears on both of your domains is just one spell; it doesn't count twice and it doesn't give you a bonus.

You can't prepare a spell that isn't on one of your domains, however much you want it. That's what a third domain is for: the **Wider Study** talent (tier 2, in both the Mind and the Soul menus) adds one more domain of a tradition you have.

Casting, upcasting a spell with a higher slot, and regaining slots on a long rest all work as the SRD says.

### Rituals, Concentration and Focus

**Rituals.** You can cast any prepared spell that has the Ritual tag as a ritual: it takes 10 minutes longer and uses no slot.

**Concentration.** The SRD rule, unchanged. One concentration spell at a time; damage calls for a Constitution save.

**Focus.** Any object that suits your tradition works as a spellcasting focus: a Thaumaturge's worn notebook, an Invoker's staff or token, whatever the player decides it is. You don't track a component pouch. The only components that matter are ones with a gold-piece cost, which you have to own (and lose, if the spell says it's consumed).

> **Example — a Priest at 1st level**
>
> A player builds a Soul character with the *Invocation* talent, chooses **Wisdom**, and takes **The Tide** and **Presence**. With Wisdom 16 she prepares 3 + 1 = **4** spells each morning from both lists: say *Cure Wounds*, *Healing Word*, *Bless* and *Command*. She has **two** 1st-level slots and knows **two** cantrips, *Spare the Dying* and *Sacred Flame*. Her spell save DC is 8 + 3 + 2 = **13**. At 3rd level she can take *Wider Study* and add Binding, and *Hold Person* goes on the list for the first time.

## Domains

A domain is a territory: the thing your magic is about. The names and territories below are the game's twenty-one canon domains (the full descriptions, with what each covers and where it stops, are in the Magic Domain Catalog appendix of the core book). What Facets d20 adds is a list of SRD 5.2.1 spells for each one. The spell's rules are the SRD's, word for word; this book only says which domain it lives in.

**You choose two domains** when you take a caster talent, from that tradition's list. Divination is the one domain on both lists.

The six domains the catalog calls **prismatic** (The Undying, Fate, The Living World, The Arcane, The Constructed Mind and Chronomancy) are open from 1st level here. Their fiction is still the widest, but their spell lists are no longer than anyone else's, and the slot table does the job of keeping the power in check.

**Table 7–5: Domains at a Glance**

| Domain | Tradition | Territory |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Invocation | Heat, flame, and light produced by fire |
| Shadow | Invocation | Darkness, absence of light, physical cold |
| Storm | Invocation | Wind, rain, lightning, concussive thunder |
| Beasts | Invocation | Communication and command of natural animals |
| Resonance | Invocation | Sound, vibration, harmonic force |
| Verdance | Invocation | Living plants: growing, shaping, animating |
| Binding | Invocation | Oaths, pacts, and spiritual constraints |
| Presence | Invocation | Projecting spiritual weight, emotional aura |
| The Tide | Invocation | Vitality flowing between living things |
| The Undying *(prismatic)* | Invocation | Death, spirits of the departed, undead |
| Fate *(prismatic)* | Invocation | Probability, luck, threads of possibility |
| The Living World *(prismatic)* | Invocation | Animating current through all living things |
| Illusion | Thaumaturgy | False sensory experience: images, sounds, smells |
| Warding | Thaumaturgy | Protective limits inscribed into space or objects |
| Inscription | Thaumaturgy | Magical effects encoded into physical marks |
| Transmutation | Thaumaturgy | Structural alteration of non-living material |
| Divination | Thaumaturgy and Invocation | Gathering information through magical means |
| Constructed Force | Thaumaturgy | Invisible force, telekinesis, barriers |
| The Arcane *(prismatic)* | Thaumaturgy | Perceiving and manipulating magic itself |
| The Constructed Mind *(prismatic)* | Thaumaturgy | Artificial intelligence and construct cognition |
| Chronomancy *(prismatic)* | Thaumaturgy | Time: acceleration, perception, brief stepping |

### Pairs for the Soul Presets

Any two domains of your tradition make a legal caster. These are the pairs the Soul presets in Chapter 05 are built around, and a sensible place to start if you're staring at the list.

**Table 7–6: Recommended Domain Pairs**

| Preset | Domains | Casting ability | What the pair gives you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Priest** | The Tide + Presence | Wisdom | Healing and restoration, blessings, courage, a radiant cantrip, *Spirit Guardians* at 5th |
| **Druid** | Verdance + Beasts | Wisdom | Plants and animals: *Entangle*, *Goodberry*, *Speak with Animals*, *Barkskin*, *Conjure Animals*, *Polymorph* |
| **Oracle** | Fate + Divination | Wisdom or Charisma | Omens and luck: *Guidance*, *Bless* and *Bane*, *Augury*, *Clairvoyance*, *Divination*, *Scrying* |

Some other shapes, if the table wants them: a **storm druid** swaps Beasts for Storm. A **graveside oracle** swaps Divination for The Undying. A **battle priest** swaps The Tide for Binding. Pairs across traditions only happen for characters who hold both talents.

> **MM Note — domains that stretch**
>
> **Default:** the lists are the lists. If a player wants a spell that isn't on their domains, the answer is Wider Study, not a ruling.
>
> **The dial:** a few SRD spells sit on a domain because they're the closest fit, not a perfect one (*Sleep* in Shadow, *Poison Spray* in Beasts, *Mending* in Chronomancy). Let the player describe the spell in their domain's terms: Shadow's *Sleep* is dark closing over the eyes, Chronomancy's *Mending* puts a thing back the way it was a minute ago. The rules text doesn't change.
>
> **The cost:** if you start swapping spells between domains at the table, the lists stop being a promise, and the Druid starts asking why the Priest got *Polymorph*.

## Domain Spell Lists

Spells are listed by name and level only. Look up what they do in the SRD 5.2.1. *Thaumaturgy* the cantrip is an SRD spell that happens to share a name with the tradition; any caster with Resonance or Presence can take it, Thaumaturge or not.

### Fire

*Invocation.* Heat, flame and fire's light. The most direct damage of any domain.

- **Cantrips:** Fire Bolt, Light, Produce Flame
- **1st:** Burning Hands, Hellish Rebuke, Searing Smite
- **2nd:** Flame Blade, Flaming Sphere, Heat Metal, Scorching Ray
- **3rd:** Fireball
- **4th:** Fire Shield, Wall of Fire
- **5th:** Flame Strike

### Shadow

*Invocation.* Darkness and physical cold. Hiding, blinding, and a cold that bites.

- **Cantrips:** Chill Touch, Ray of Frost
- **1st:** Fog Cloud, Sleep
- **2nd:** Blindness/Deafness, Darkness, Darkvision, Pass without Trace
- **3rd:** Nondetection, Sleet Storm
- **4th:** Black Tentacles, Ice Storm
- **5th:** Cone of Cold

### Storm

*Invocation.* Wind, rain, lightning and thunder. Loud, wide and hard to stand in.

- **Cantrips:** Druidcraft, Elementalism, Shocking Grasp
- **1st:** Feather Fall, Fog Cloud, Thunderwave
- **2nd:** Gust of Wind, Shatter
- **3rd:** Call Lightning, Fly, Lightning Bolt, Wind Wall
- **5th:** Conjure Elemental

### Beasts

*Invocation.* Natural animals: speaking with them, calling them, becoming like them. Never people, never undead or constructs.

- **Cantrips:** Druidcraft, Poison Spray
- **1st:** Animal Friendship, Hunter's Mark, Speak with Animals
- **2nd:** Animal Messenger, Enhance Ability, Locate Animals or Plants
- **3rd:** Conjure Animals
- **4th:** Dominate Beast, Giant Insect, Polymorph
- **5th:** Awaken, Insect Plague

### Resonance

*Invocation.* Sound and vibration. It can deafen, shatter, frighten and carry a voice a long way, but it cannot command. The list stops at 3rd level; a Resonance caster wants a second domain with some reach.

- **Cantrips:** Message, Thaumaturgy, Vicious Mockery
- **1st:** Thunderwave
- **2nd:** Blindness/Deafness, Enthrall, Knock, Shatter, Silence
- **3rd:** Fear, Sending, Tongues

### Verdance

*Invocation.* Living plants, rooted in soil or water.

- **Cantrips:** Druidcraft, Shillelagh
- **1st:** Ensnaring Strike, Entangle, Goodberry
- **2nd:** Barkskin, Locate Animals or Plants, Spike Growth
- **3rd:** Plant Growth, Speak with Plants
- **4th:** Blight
- **5th:** Awaken, Tree Stride

### Binding

*Invocation.* Oaths, pacts and constraints: holding a creature still, keeping a promise honest, putting a thing back where it belongs.

- **Cantrips:** Mending, Resistance
- **1st:** Bane, Protection from Evil and Good, Sanctuary
- **2nd:** Hold Person, Warding Bond, Zone of Truth
- **3rd:** Bestow Curse, Magic Circle, Remove Curse
- **4th:** Banishment
- **5th:** Geas, Hold Monster, Planar Binding

### Presence

*Invocation.* Spiritual weight and emotional aura: courage, calm, fear and the sense that someone is watching over you.

- **Cantrips:** Sacred Flame, Thaumaturgy
- **1st:** Bless, Command, Divine Smite, Heroism, Shield of Faith
- **2nd:** Aid, Calm Emotions, Spiritual Weapon
- **3rd:** Beacon of Hope, Fear, Spirit Guardians
- **4th:** Guardian of Faith
- **5th:** Dispel Evil and Good

### The Tide

*Invocation.* Vitality flowing between living things, in both directions. *Revivify* and *Raise Dead* are here because the catalog puts the restoring of life at The Tide's absolute limits. Both keep their SRD costs.

- **Cantrips:** Resistance, Spare the Dying
- **1st:** Cure Wounds, False Life, Healing Word, Inflict Wounds
- **2nd:** Lesser Restoration, Prayer of Healing
- **3rd:** Mass Healing Word, Revivify, Vampiric Touch
- **4th:** Aura of Life
- **5th:** Greater Restoration, Mass Cure Wounds, Raise Dead

### The Undying

*Invocation. Prismatic.* Death, spirits of the departed, and the undead. Not healing.

- **Cantrips:** Chill Touch, Spare the Dying
- **1st:** Detect Evil and Good, False Life, Inflict Wounds
- **2nd:** Gentle Repose, Ray of Enfeeblement
- **3rd:** Animate Dead, Speak with Dead, Vampiric Touch
- **4th:** Blight, Death Ward
- **5th:** Contagion

### Fate

*Invocation. Prismatic.* Probability, luck and the threads of what might happen. It nudges; it never compels.

- **Cantrips:** Guidance, Resistance, Starry Wisp, True Strike
- **1st:** Bane, Bless
- **2nd:** Augury
- **3rd:** Bestow Curse, Blink
- **4th:** Death Ward, Divination
- **5th:** Commune

### The Living World

*Invocation. Prismatic.* The current that animates all living things, plant and beast alike.

- **Cantrips:** Druidcraft, Guidance
- **1st:** Cure Wounds, Goodberry, Longstrider, Speak with Animals
- **2nd:** Locate Animals or Plants, Pass without Trace
- **3rd:** Plant Growth, Speak with Plants
- **4th:** Freedom of Movement, Polymorph
- **5th:** Antilife Shell, Awaken, Commune with Nature

### Illusion

*Thaumaturgy.* False sights, sounds and sensations. A careful creature can see through them.

- **Cantrips:** Dancing Lights, Minor Illusion
- **1st:** Color Spray, Disguise Self, Illusory Script, Silent Image
- **2nd:** Blur, Invisibility, Mirror Image
- **3rd:** Hypnotic Pattern, Major Image
- **4th:** Greater Invisibility, Hallucinatory Terrain, Phantasmal Killer
- **5th:** Seeming

### Warding

*Thaumaturgy.* Protective limits anchored in a place or an object, rather than a moving creature.

- **Cantrips:** Mending, Resistance
- **1st:** Alarm, Protection from Evil and Good
- **2nd:** Arcane Lock
- **3rd:** Glyph of Warding, Magic Circle, Nondetection, Protection from Energy, Tiny Hut
- **4th:** Faithful Hound, Private Sanctum
- **5th:** Dispel Evil and Good, Hallow

### Inscription

*Thaumaturgy.* Effects written into marks: runes, scripts, glyphs and circles.

- **Cantrips:** Mending, Prestidigitation
- **1st:** Comprehend Languages, Identify, Illusory Script
- **2nd:** Arcane Lock, Continual Flame, Magic Mouth, Magic Weapon
- **3rd:** Glyph of Warding
- **4th:** Secret Chest
- **5th:** Teleportation Circle

### Transmutation

*Thaumaturgy.* Changing the structure of non-living material. It alters; it doesn't create from nothing.

- **Cantrips:** Acid Splash, Mending, Prestidigitation
- **1st:** Grease, Purify Food and Drink
- **2nd:** Enlarge/Reduce, Knock
- **3rd:** Meld into Stone
- **4th:** Fabricate, Stone Shape, Stoneskin
- **5th:** Passwall, Wall of Stone

### Divination

*Thaumaturgy and Invocation.* Finding out. It sees what is or what has been, not the inside of someone's head. The only domain both traditions share: a Thaumaturge works it out, an Invoker is shown.

- **Cantrips:** Guidance, True Strike
- **1st:** Comprehend Languages, Detect Magic, Detect Poison and Disease, Identify
- **2nd:** Find Traps, Locate Object, See Invisibility
- **3rd:** Clairvoyance
- **4th:** Arcane Eye, Divination, Locate Creature
- **5th:** Legend Lore, Scrying

### Constructed Force

*Thaumaturgy.* Invisible kinetic force: hands, shields, walls and blunt pressure. Not fire, not lightning.

- **Cantrips:** Eldritch Blast, Mage Hand
- **1st:** Mage Armor, Magic Missile, Shield, Unseen Servant
- **2nd:** Levitate
- **4th:** Resilient Sphere
- **5th:** Arcane Hand, Telekinesis, Wall of Force

### The Arcane

*Thaumaturgy. Prismatic.* Magic itself: seeing it, reading it, cancelling it.

- **Cantrips:** Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, Sorcerous Burst
- **1st:** Detect Magic, Identify
- **2nd:** See Invisibility
- **3rd:** Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Nondetection, Remove Curse
- **4th:** Banishment
- **5th:** Dispel Evil and Good, Legend Lore

### The Constructed Mind

*Thaumaturgy. Prismatic.* Artificial minds: servants, familiars, guardians and things made to move on their own.

- **Cantrips:** Mage Hand, Mending
- **1st:** Find Familiar, Unseen Servant
- **2nd:** Magic Mouth
- **3rd:** Phantom Steed
- **4th:** Fabricate, Faithful Hound
- **5th:** Animate Objects, Arcane Hand

### Chronomancy

*Thaumaturgy. Prismatic.* Time in small handfuls: faster, slower, a step out of the moment, a glimpse a little way ahead or behind.

- **Cantrips:** Mending, True Strike
- **1st:** Expeditious Retreat, Feather Fall
- **2nd:** Gentle Repose
- **3rd:** Blink, Haste, Slow
- **4th:** Divination
- **5th:** Legend Lore

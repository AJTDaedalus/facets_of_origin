*   **System:** *Facets of Origin* (Pre-Release Version)
*   **Mirror Master (MM):** Arthur (Novice)
*   **Players:**
    *   Sophia (Expert) playing **Serane Vaskarin** (Mind-focused Scion)
    *   Penny (Novice) playing **Pello the Phern** (Dexterity-focused Rogue)
    *   Luke (Expert) playing **Dassa the House-Blade** (Strength-focused Guardian)
    *   Toby (Novice) playing **Ilesse Kethaun** (Wisdom-focused Investigator)

## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement II - Ballroom Networking)

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, you are inside the ballroom. The music is playing. There are two hundred masked guests drinking wine. You want to accomplish your agendas. Serane, you want to learn about Raunu's secret decree, the 'Tithe of Hands'. Pello, you want to find out about the river gate. Dassa, you want to find your sister Anha. Ilesse, you want to get word to Veier.
Uh, so what do you do? Go around the room.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll circulate around the buffet table, listening for Minister Corval's assistants. Serane strikes up a conversation with a junior scribe. 'The vintage is exquisite tonight. Corval must have spent months preparing House Boranis' return.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Scribe check. Persuade roll. The scribe is wary, so this is a Hard check (-1). Roll intelligence + Persuade.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Social Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Social check (Hard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 1 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. That's a failure. The scribe looks at your mask, narrows his eyes, and says: "I don't talk to Vaskarins." He walks away. You learn nothing.

> **Penny (Pello):** "Let me try. I'll head toward the kitchen doors. I want to see if I can overhear any carters or servants talking about the river gate key or who has it."

**Arthur (MM):** Stealth check to eavesdrop by the kitchen. The kitchen is busy and there are guards. Hard check (-1). Roll Dexterity + Stealth.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Social Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Social check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. The chief steward catches you standing too close to the doors. "Hey! Guests aren't allowed in the service corridor! Get back to the dance floor." He pushes you back. You can't go near the kitchens again tonight.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa watches Pello get shoved. She walks up to the steward, towering over him, and asks bluntly: 'Where is Anha? She works the kitchens. I have a message from her mother.'"

**Arthur (MM):** The steward is annoyed now. Intimidate check. Hard difficulty (-1). Roll Strength.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Social Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Social check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 4) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6. The steward flags a Boranis house guard. "This sentinel-masked guest is harassing the staff. Watch her." The guard stands next to Dassa, preventing her from moving freely.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Oh dear. I'll try to find Maiven Nolonaire's contact in the gallery. I'll slide along the wall and look for someone wearing the silver-hawk brooch."

**Arthur (MM):** The gallery is warded and watched. Hard check (-1) to find the contact secretly. Roll Wisdom + Persuade.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Social Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Social check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 0 = **6** (Failure).

**Arthur (MM):** A 6! A total sweep of failures. The gallery guards step forward and block the stairs. "The upper level is closed for private meetings. Turn back."

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Arthur, on a 6-, the rules say you should advance the threat clock or deploy consequences, not just block us from playing the game. The night is moving forward, so the situation should complicate."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Uh... right, let me check the book. Page 40... Okay, because of all the failures, the 'Sect Suspicion' Threat Clock advances by one segment. It's now 1/4. And everyone feels the eyes of the ballroom on them. It feels like the party is closing in."

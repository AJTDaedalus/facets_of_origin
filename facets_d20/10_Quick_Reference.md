## Levels (Chapter 02)

- Levels 1–10. Proficiency +2 (1st–4th), +3 (5th–8th), +4 (9th–10th).
- One talent every level from 2nd. Tier 1 from 1st, tier 2 from 3rd, tier 3 from 6th.
- Signature at 3rd, from your Facet's six. Not a talent pick.
- Ability Score Improvement is a tier 1 talent on every menu: +2 to one or +1 to two, max 20.
- Cross-Facet from 2nd: another Facet's tier 1 costs one pick; its tier 2–3 need two talents from that Facet first.
- Your own tradition's talent: full caster. The other tradition's, or either as Body: half caster. Both tables are read by caster level, counting from the level you took the talent (Chapter 07).

## Backgrounds (Chapter 06)

+2/+1 or +1/+1/+1 to three listed abilities · two skills · one tool · one origin talent (any Facet, doesn't use a pick) · a Specialty · the tool and 25 gp.

**Specialty:** advantage when it applies; routine things inside it need no roll.

## Social (Chapter 06)

**Hostile → Wary → Neutral → Friendly → Ally.** What their attitude allows, you get by asking. DC 10 / 15 / 20 by how hard they are to move.

**Table 10–3: Social Results**

| Result | Attitude |
|---|---|
| Beat DC by 10+ | Two steps toward Ally |
| Meet or beat DC | One step toward Ally |
| Miss by 1–4 | No change; that argument is spent this scene |
| Miss by 5+ | One step toward Hostile |

Leverage or a fitting Specialty: advantage.

## Combat (Chapter 08)

**Table 10–4: The Round**

| Step | |
|---|---|
| Who goes first | Surprise, or: the party's best Dexterity check vs the MM's one roll. Ties go to the players. Once per fight. |
| Each side's half | Every creature on the side takes a whole turn, in any order the side likes. |

- **Your turn:** move, one action, a bonus action if you have one, one free object interaction.
- **Actions:** Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic, Ready, Search, Study, Utilize.
- **Reaction:** one per round. Leaving an enemy's reach on your own movement provokes one opportunity attack.
- **Cover:** half +2, three-quarters +5, total can't be targeted.
- **Monster damage:** fixed. On a critical hit, the MM doubles the dice and rolls.
- **Bloodied:** at or below half HP.
- **Morale:** non-boss foes make a DC 10 Wis save when first Bloodied and when their leader falls. Fail: flee, surrender or bargain. The mindless and the bound never break.
- **Minions:** 1 HP. Any hit kills. A successful save takes no damage.
- **Bosses:** two turns per side's half. Change when Bloodied. No morale checks.
- **At 0 HP:** unconscious; SRD death saves (DC 10, three successes or three failures).

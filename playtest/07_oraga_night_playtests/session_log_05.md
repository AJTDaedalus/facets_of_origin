# Playtest Session Log 05: The East Wing Retreat

*   **System:** *Facets of Origin* (Pre-Release Version)
*   **Mirror Master (MM):** Arthur (Novice)
*   **Players:**
    *   Sophia (Expert) playing **Serane Vaskarin** (Mind-focused Scion)
    *   Penny (Novice) playing **Pello the Phern** (Dexterity-focused Rogue)
    *   Luke (Expert) playing **Dassa the House-Blade** (Strength-focused Guardian)
    *   Toby (Novice) playing **Ilesse Kethaun** (Wisdom-focused Investigator)

---

## Scene: The East Wing Corridor (Movement VII - The Escape)

**Arthur (MM):** You have run into the east wing. Wards are flaring and corridors are closing. The Radiant (TR 12) is standing at the end of the hall, throwing fire. You see Veier Nolonaire (the bride) leaning against a pillar, clutching her stomach in pain. Wounded guards lie around her.
The Radiant is preparing to shoot a massive ball of fire down the hallway at Veier and you. What do you do?

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I want to cast a Warding spell to create a crystal shield blocking the fire! I'll hold my crystal out and direct the magical flow. Serane yells: 'Anchor the lattice! Hold the barrier!'"
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Wait. A Warding spell? There's no spell list in the manual for 'crystal shield'. How many points of damage does it block? What's the casting time? Let me look at the manual... page 90... I think you can only cast basic light or shield spells from a template."
>
> **Sophia (Serane):** "No, Arthur. The magic system in *Facets of Origin* is freeform. I describe my intent—to weave a crystal shield to block the corridor—and my scope. Since it's blocking a single hallway for a single attack, it's a **Minor** scope intent. Because I don't have a specific Technique yet, it becomes a **Hard** check (-1 modifier). I roll Mind + Lore."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Uh... okay, let's make it a Hard check. But if you fail, the fire hits you. Roll."

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Cast Warding]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Cast Warding magic (Hard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 1 = **11** (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 11. That's a full success. A crystal wall flares up, blocking the fire. The fire splashes harmlessly against the crystals.
But the Radiant starts humming a high-pitched note, preparing a sound burst to shatter the crystal wall!

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I want to cast a Resonance spell! I want to vibrate the air around the Radiant, creating a counter-resonant hum that cancels out its note and staggers it."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Vibrating air? That's not a spell. You should just throw a rock or strike it. How do I know if that's allowed? Let me check the index... okay, I guess Resonance can do sound. But since this is a counter-spell, it's a **Hard** check (-1). Roll Spirit + Attune."
>
> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Rolling on the server..."

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Cast Resonance]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Cast Resonance magic (Hard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 1 = **11** (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 11! Another full success. Your counter-resonant scream matches the Radiant's hum. A loud *POP* echoes, and the Radiant's focus is broken. It staggers back, clutch-masked.
But Veier Nolonaire is still in pain. "We must reach the river gate. The baby is coming..."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "I'll lift Veier onto my shoulder. 'Hold on, Lady Nolonaire. Dassa has you.' Pello, open the side door. Let's move!"
>
> **Penny (Pello):** "I'll lead the way, checking for traps!"
>
> **Arthur (MM):** Okay, you run down the back stairs toward the river gate. The session ends as you escape into the cold night.

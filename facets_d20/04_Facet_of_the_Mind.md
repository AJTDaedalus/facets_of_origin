# Facet of the Mind

The Mind is the Facet of people who look before they act, and then act on what they saw: the wizard with a book of formulae, the investigator who knows which rib to aim between, the tinker whose construct holds the door. Its tradition is **Thaumaturgy**, the scholarly magic, cast with Intelligence. Its hit die is the smallest, so a Mind character is usually standing behind someone, and it has more talents to choose from than either other Facet.

Zahna is Mind. He would like it noted that he read this chapter first.

## Mind Features

### Study
*Mind feature · 1st level*

As a bonus action, study a creature you can see within 60 feet. It is your **studied target** until the fight ends or you study another. You learn its resistances, immunities and vulnerabilities, and your next attack roll or check against it this turn has advantage. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus; all uses return on a short rest.

This feature is separate from the SRD's Study action, which everyone still has.

### Studied Recovery
*Mind feature · 2nd level*

If you cast spells: once per long rest, when you finish a short rest, recover spent spell slots whose levels add up to half your level, rounded up. None of them can be 5th level.

Three Steel talents, four Spell talents and six general ones. To read an entry, see *Reading a talent entry* in Chapter 02.

### Evoker
*Spell talent · Mind*

When you cast an area spell, choose up to 1 + the spell's level creatures in its area: they automatically succeed on its save and take no damage from it. Add your casting modifier to one damage roll of each damaging spell you cast, cantrips included.

**9th (Spell 3):** once per long rest, a damaging spell you cast deals the maximum on every die.

### Clockwork Guardian
*Spell talent · Mind*

You build and animate a small construct: AC 15, hit points 5 × your level, speed 30 feet. It never attacks. It acts right after you, and on its turn it either **Helps** (an ally's next attack against a creature within 5 feet of it has advantage) or **Guards** (until your next turn, the first hit on an ally within 5 feet of it deals 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier less damage). If it is destroyed, you rebuild it on a long rest. It is your one controlled creature (Chapter 08).

### Anatomist
*Steel talent · Mind*

You know where to strike. Your weapon attacks against your studied target deal extra damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (not a rider) and score a critical hit on a 19 or 20. When you score a critical hit against it, it is also knocked prone.

**9th (Steel 3):** the critical range against your studied target is 18–20.

### Anticipate
*General talent · Mind*

When your studied target makes an attack roll against you or a creature you can see within 30 feet, you can use your reaction to give it disadvantage. There is no limit beyond your one reaction a round.

### Iron Mind
*General talent · Mind*

You have advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened.

**5th:** when you succeed on a save against a spell, you take no damage from it.
**9th:** you have advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saves.

### Master Plan
*General talent · Mind*

Once per short rest, before a fight you had a scene to plan for, lay out the plan. In the first round of that fight, you and each ally who follows the plan have advantage on attack rolls and saving throws.

**5th:** the plan holds through the second round too.

### Field Kit
*General talent · Mind*

After each long rest you rig the party's gear. At the start of each fight, you and up to your proficiency bonus of allies gain temporary hit points equal to half your level, rounded up.

## Example: Zahna Studies the Door Guard

*The party has reached the lower archive. The MM has described the construct standing guard in front of the far door, motionless.*

**Zahna:** I study it. Bonus action.

**MM:** *Study*, so no roll. It is immune to poison damage and can't be charmed. It has no vulnerabilities.

**Zahna:** Then nobody bother shouting at it. Mordai.

**Mordai:** I wasn't going to shout.

**MM:** (He was absolutely going to shout.)

→ The construct is now Zahna's studied target. His next attack roll or check against it this turn has advantage, and the party now knows not to waste poison on it.

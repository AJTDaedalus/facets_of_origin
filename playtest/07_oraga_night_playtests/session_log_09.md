## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement VI - The Unmasking)

**Cyrus (MM):** The lights gutter and die. A cold wind sweeps through the Crystal Court. The Wept (TR 18) crosses the hall toward Raunu. The Hollow (TR 7) blocks the doors, her shadow lashes keeping the guests penned in.
Let's enter combat. Declare your postures, players.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa declares **Defensive** posture. She steps in front of Pello, her greatsword ready. I want to intercept any incoming lashes targeting him. My reaction cost is discounted by 1."
>
> **Penny (Pello):** "Pello goes **Aggressive**! I draw my smuggler's daggers, leap onto the banister, and strike at the Hollow's throat! That gives me +1 to my attack."
>
> **Sophia (Serane):** "Serane declares **Measured** posture. She will stay back and watch for openings."
>
> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse goes **Withdrawn** posture to recover some energy and watch the exits."

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent. The Hollow lashes out. Pello strikes at the Hollow. Let's resolve Pello's strike. Aggressive posture (+1) + Dexterity (+1) + Combat (+0) = +2 modifier. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Strike]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Pello, your dagger cuts the Hollow's shoulder, but the shadow lashes whip back, lashing at you simultaneously.
Pello, you must react. You choose to Dodge. Dodge costs 1 Endurance normally, but in Aggressive posture it costs **2 Endurance**. Roll Dexterity + Dodge. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Dodge]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Dodge roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 1 = **6** (Failure).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 6! Failure. The shadow lash catches you in mid-air, slamming you into the obsidian floor. You take a strike (2 damage) and are Prone (Tier 1 Condition). Pello reduces his Endurance by 2 (from 3 to **1**).
Now let's resolve Dassa's action. Dassa strikes the Hollow. Defensive posture (-1) + Strength (+1) + Combat (+1) = +1 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Strike]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 4) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Your greatsword chips the Hollow's shadow-skin, but its companion strike lashes back at you.
Dassa, you choose to Parry. Parry costs 1 Endurance, but in Defensive posture it costs **0 Endurance**. Roll Strength + Combat. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Parry]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Parry roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 2) + 2 = **6** (Failure).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 6! Failure. Dassa, your greatsword is knocked aside. The shadow lash slices across your chest. A standard strike deals 2 damage. But you have **Heavy Armor (Tier 2)**, which downgrades the damage by 2. You take **0 damage** to your Endurance, spending one of your armor charges. You have 1 armor downgrade remaining.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "That heavy armor is a lifesaver. Pello, get up! We need to pull back."
>
> **Penny (Pello):** "Ouch... my ribs. I'm moving!"
>
> **Cyrus (MM):** The exchange ends. The room is filling with smoke.

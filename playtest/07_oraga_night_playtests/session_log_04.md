# Playtest Session Log 04: Ballroom Midnight Clashes

*   **System:** *Facets of Origin* (Pre-Release Version)
*   **Mirror Master (MM):** Arthur (Novice)
*   **Players:**
    *   Sophia (Expert) playing **Serane Vaskarin** (Mind-focused Scion)
    *   Penny (Novice) playing **Pello the Phern** (Dexterity-focused Rogue)
    *   Luke (Expert) playing **Dassa the House-Blade** (Strength-focused Guardian)
    *   Toby (Novice) playing **Ilesse Kethaun** (Wisdom-focused Investigator)

---

## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement VI - The Unmasking)

**Arthur (MM):** Midnight strikes. The bells ring. The east doors open, and Raunu Boranis walks onto the dais. He raises a cup. But suddenly, the lights die. Not just blown out — the crystal lights go cold and quiet in your hands. People start screaming.
Three gray masks stay on. The Hollow (TR 7) blocks the main doors!
Uh, let's roll initiative for combat! Everyone roll a d20.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Arthur, remember: *Facets of Origin* does not use initiative rolls or d20s. Combat is resolved in simultaneous exchanges. All players declare their postures first, then the MM resolves actions and reactions together."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Oh! Right. Let me check the rules... page 45. Postures. Okay. What are your postures?"
>
> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa declares a **Defensive** posture. She draws her greatsword, stepping in front of Pello and Serane to shield them from the Hollow's sweeping shadow lashes."
>
> **Penny (Pello):** "Pello declares a **Measured** posture. I draw my daggers and prepare to strike."
>
> **Sophia (Serane):** "Serane declares a **Measured** posture. She backs away, holding her uncharged soul-crystal."
>
> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I'll go **Measured** too. I want to search for the exit."

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, so the Hollow strikes at Pello. Pello, what do you do?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to strike back! Pello dashes forward and stabs at the Hollow's side."

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, so Pello strikes, and then it is Dassa's turn... wait, no, they happen at the same time. Let's roll Pello's strike. Standard difficulty. Roll Dexterity + Combat.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Strike]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 1 = **11** (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** Wow, an 11! That's a full success. You hit the Hollow, dealing a strike (2 damage). The Hollow has 7 Resolve. It drops to 5.
Wait, now the Hollow attacks back at Pello. Pello, you have to react.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to Dodge!"

**Arthur (MM):** Dodge costs 1 Endurance. Pello, you are in Measured posture, so it is just 1. Roll Dexterity + Dodge.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Dodge]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Dodge roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (6, 6) + 1 = **13** (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 13! Full success. You flip over the shadow lash cleanly. No damage taken.
Now it's Dassa's turn... I mean, Dassa's action. Dassa, you are in Defensive posture. What are you doing?

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa strikes the Hollow with her greatsword, trying to draw its attention away from Pello."

**Arthur (MM):** Dassa strikes. In Defensive posture, she gets a -1 modifier. Roll Strength + Combat.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Strike]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier after Defensive posture penalty)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 1 = **11** (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 11! Another full success. Your greatsword smashes into the shadow, dealing another strike (2 damage). The Hollow drops to 3 Resolve.
And the Hollow sweeps its arm at Dassa. Dassa, react.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa chooses to Parry. In Defensive posture, Parry costs 0 Endurance. Rolling Strength + Combat."

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Parry]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Parry roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 6) + 2 = **13** (Full Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 13! Full success. You deflect the blow with your greatsword. No damage taken.
Uh... okay, the exchange ends. The Hollow retreats into the crowd, herding guests away. The corridor is open.

> **Luke (Dassa):** "That simultaneous exchange saved us a lot of damage. Let's move to the east wing before more guards arrive."

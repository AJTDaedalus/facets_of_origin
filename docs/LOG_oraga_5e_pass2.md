My model is harder on the Attendant than Steel's was. With his S-1 block (HP 178) and
§5b as first written, I get blows-only 49% / all-down 37% for the pregens, bare
distraction 99%, leaning in 100%. The distraction game as written was not a fight.
No block change alone fixes that: with advantage on top of a +8 skill and as many
tries a round as the table has characters, the DC stops mattering. So the levers moved:

- **One trick a round**, and Help doesn't apply (with Help for advantage, leaning in
  jumps from 72% to 88%).
- **Leaning in is +2 / +2, +4 at most** (in character; a habit the party has seen).
  No advantage, no novelty bonus. Repeats still have disadvantage and never work a
  third time. The "noticed it before midnight → advantage" line is gone: the habit
  +2 is that reward now.
- **Only a broken focus counts, and it takes four.** A success while Idle costs it a
  turn but doesn't count.
- **Quicker refocus:** a broken focus lasts until the start of its next turn; it
  refocuses then if any Uninvited in the scene has no Delay. Delay on all of them keeps

# Taven Olmarin — The Rising Artist *(Orthaen)*

A Rekuzan musician on the way up, with a card in the chief's own hand and every
intention of leaving the ball with a name the whole city knows.

**Bard (College of Lore) 4** · Human (Orthaen, gifted) · Background: Rising Artist

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 (−1) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) |

**Armor Class** 15 (Studded Leather Armor, under a performer's festival coat) ·
**Hit Points** 27 · **Hit Dice** 4d8 · **Initiative** +3 · **Speed** 30 ft. ·
**Passive Perception** 13

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Cha +6

**Skills** Performance +8, Persuasion +8, Deception +6, Intimidation +6, Acrobatics +5,
Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5, Insight +3, Medicine +3, Perception +3, History +2,
Investigation +2; with Jack of All Trades, Animal Handling +2, Survival +2, Arcana +1,
Nature +1, Religion +1, Athletics +0

**Tools** Disguise Kit; three musical instruments of the player's choice

**Background — Rising Artist.** *Abilities:* Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma (+2 Cha, +1
Dex). *Origin feat:* Skilled (History, Sleight of Hand, Stealth). *Skills:* Performance,
Persuasion. *Tool:* Disguise Kit.
**Specialty:** the city's stages: who books whom, which patrons pay, and which rooms a
song carries in. When it bears, the DM gives the answer without a check.

**Feats.** *Skilled* (background). *Orthaen Gift* (Versatile): Advantage on checks to
coax, shape, read or judge grown crystal and any working held in it; knows
*Prestidigitation* (Charisma), worked through a sliver of crystal. *Ability Score
Improvement* (4th level): Charisma +1, Dexterity +1 (included).

**Attacks**
- *Dagger (his knife).* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or thrown 20/60 ft., 1d4 + 3 Piercing.
- *Vicious Mockery.* One creature he can see or hear within 60 ft.: it must succeed on
  a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or take 1d6 Psychic damage and have Disadvantage on the
  next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

**Class Features**
- **Bardic Inspiration** (4 uses, d6; regain on a Long Rest). As a Bonus Action, he can
  inspire another creature within 60 feet of himself who can see or hear him. That
  creature gains one Bardic Inspiration die, and once within the next hour when it
  fails a D20 Test, it can roll the die and add the number rolled, potentially turning
  the failure into a success.
- **Expertise** in Performance and Persuasion. **Jack of All Trades.**
- **Cutting Words** (College of Lore). Reaction, when a creature he can see within 60
  ft. makes a damage roll or succeeds on an ability check or attack roll: spend a
  Bardic Inspiration and subtract the roll from its total.
- **Bonus Proficiencies** (College of Lore): Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine.

**Spellcasting** (Charisma; save DC 14, attack +6; a musical instrument as his focus)
- *Slots:* 1st ×4, 2nd ×3
- *Cantrips:* *Message*, *Minor Illusion*, *Vicious Mockery*; *Prestidigitation* (gift)
- *Prepared:* *Charm Person*, *Comprehend Languages*, *Healing Word*, *Hideous Laughter*,
  *Silent Image*; *2nd:* *Calm Emotions*, *Suggestion*

**Carrying.** Dagger · Fine Clothes · Studded Leather Armor · a musical instrument
(his focus) · Disguise Kit · crystal charge: *Steady Light* · 60 GP · Heroic Inspiration

**Personality.** A born crowd-reader who plays every room like it's the gig that makes
him. Tonight is the biggest stage in Rekuzan, and he means to be remembered for it.

**How he got in.** Raunu's own card, sent for by name (the Invited). Word got round,
and the Thenya delegation hired him on top of it (the Patron's Errand): the only
non-Orthaen faces in fifty can't get close to anyone, and a local who was invited on his
own merit can. *Suggested agenda:* The Cousin's Errand (Agenda 4), with the
grandmother's ring in his pocket.

**Mask.** *What spirit-face does Taven wear on the biggest stage of his life, and who
does he hope will recognize him?*

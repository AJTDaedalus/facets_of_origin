## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement II - Networking)

**Cyrus (MM):** The warm light of the crystal sconces glints off the black obsidian floor. Among the flurry of serving staff, you notice a steward in Boranis livery adjusting a crystal tray. Her movements are quiet, precise, and completely unremarkable—except that she lingers near the door to the wester-gallery, placing a warded crystal seal on the frame. It's Tavva.
Pello, Serane, how do you handle this?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to bump into her 'accidentally' and see what she's placing. Pello slips his fox mask closer, holding a glass of punch. 'Pardon, steward. Exquisite decorations tonight.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent cover. Since she is focused on her work, this is a Standard difficulty check. Pello rolls Dexterity.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Social Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Pello, you bump into her. She catches the tray with perfect reflexes, but your hand brushes her sleeve and you feel a hard warded pass-key crystal in her pocket. She looks at you, her eyes narrowing behind her serving posture. "A clumsy move, guest. But... perhaps we can trade. I know you are looking for the river gate key. Master Vell has it. But if you tell Corval I am here, my crew will seal the gallery gates shut."
You know who has the key, but she knows you are onto her.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll step up, sliding a silver coin onto her tray. 'A minor spill, steward. Let us discuss the Vaskarin crystal in the gallery. My family wants it back.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Serane, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Social Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Tavva whispers: "The Vaskarin crystal is in case B6. I am hitting it tonight. If you let my crew handle the guards, I will leave the crystal wester-gate unlocked for you after we take the other pieces. Deal? But we must work fast—my scout says the inner guards are suspicious."
So you have a deal, but the guard suspicion rises.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse steps up, checking her hawk mask. 'And our sister Anha?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Ilesse, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Social Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 2 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Tavva nods. "Anha is in the pantry next to B6. She is safe. I will let her guide you through the service tunnels at midnight, but you must help my scout slip past the gallery guards first."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa grunts. 'I'll handle the guards.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Dassa, roll Strength. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Social Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Social check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 8. Partial success. You talk to the guard sergeant, drawing him away. "The sentinel-masked guest is asking about security," he grunts. The guards are distracted, but they note Dassa's description, giving her the 'Noticed' condition. Tavva's scout slips through the gate.

# Playtest Session Log 01: The Gates of Boranis

*   **System:** *Facets of Origin* (Pre-Release Version)
*   **Mirror Master (MM):** Arthur (Novice)
*   **Players:**
    *   Sophia (Expert) playing **Serane Vaskarin** (Mind-focused Scion)
    *   Penny (Novice) playing **Pello the Phern** (Dexterity-focused Rogue)
    *   Luke (Expert) playing **Dassa the House-Blade** (Strength-focused Guardian)
    *   Toby (Novice) playing **Ilesse Kethaun** (Wisdom-focused Investigator)

---

## Scene: The Outer Gates of House Boranis

**Arthur (MM):** Uh, okay. Welcome to the first playtest. You guys are standing in front of the outer gates of House Boranis. The gates are shut. There are three guards wearing bronze armor. They have spears. They look at you through their masks. Uh, before anyone says anything, let's roll initiative. Everyone roll 2d6 to see who talks first.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Wait, Arthur. *Facets of Origin* doesn't use initiative rolls, especially not in social situations. We can just speak naturally or explain what our characters are doing, and then the MM decides who has the spotlight."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Oh, right. Let me look at the table of contents... page 12. Pacing. Right, no initiative. Okay, so Serane, what are you doing?"
>
> **Sophia (Serane):** "Serane adjusts her porcelain Weaver mask, steps forward, and presents our invitation to the lead guard. 'House Boranis opened its doors to friends tonight. Surely the Vaskarin lineage is welcome inside.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, that's a Persuade check. You are Mind-focused, so roll 2d6 + your Persuade skill + Charisma. What's your Charisma?

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Charisma is 3, so +1 modifier. Persuade is Practiced, so that's another +1 rank modifier. Total modifier is +2. Standard difficulty. Rolling on the server..."

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Persuade roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 2 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 7. That's... a success, but with a cost. Let's see. The guard looks at the invitation, nods, but says: "This is a Vaskarin invitation, yes. But it lists three guests, and there are four of you. One of you has to stay outside."

> **Penny (Pello):** "Wait! I have an invitation too! Pello steps forward, slipping his bronze fox mask slightly. 'I am Pello. My uncle from the Merchant's Circle sent me. Let me check my invitation...'"

**Arthur (MM):** Pello, make a check to show your invitation. That's a Finesse check to slip it out of your cloak smoothly. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Finesse roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 1 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** Another 7. A partial success. The guard takes Pello's invite. "This is valid, but it has mud all over it. I can't read the stamp. You can enter, but we must confiscate your Phern danger-sense daggers at the door as a precaution."

> **Penny (Pello):** "Aww, my daggers? But they're family heirlooms! Dassa, help me out."
>
> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa steps between Pello and the guards. She doesn't draw her heavy blade, but she crosses her arms, letting her plain sentinel mask loom over the lead guard. 'The Phern is with us. His steel stays on him. We are guests of House Boranis, not prisoners.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Dassa, that's a show of force. Roll Strength + Combat. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Intimidate roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 2 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** Another 7! Okay, the guard grunts. "Fine. He keeps the daggers. But because you argued, the sergeant has marked your names on the ledger. The inner guards will keep an eye on you all night. You have the 'Watched' condition (Tier 1)."

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Wait, let me try to smooth this over before we walk in. Ilesse steps up, her hawk mask glinting. 'My good guard, we are simply excited for the festival. Here, a small Orthaen crystal token of our goodwill. Let us forget this unpleasantness.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Ilesse, make a Persuade roll. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Persuade roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (2, 3) + 2 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 7 again! Wow, everyone rolled a 7. The guard accepts the crystal. "Alright, I'll scratch your names off the suspicious list. But... the crystal token you gave me is a warded light crystal. The light flares brightly as I take it, and it attracts the attention of a rival noble, Minister Vorlain's factor, who is standing nearby. He watches you walk in and notes your faces. You get the 'Noticed by Vorlain' condition."

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Well, at least we are inside the gates. Let's head into the Crystal Court."

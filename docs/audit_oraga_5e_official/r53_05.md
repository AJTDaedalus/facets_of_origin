## What was done

- **Tics cut by rewording, with no synonym tics.** For example, "worth exactly as much" became "worth the same". The two "exactly what to touch" lines became "which one to touch" and "what to touch". "Understand exactly" became "Be clear". "the whole burning room" became "the burning room". "the whole night points" became "the night has been pointing". "quietly lower the numbers" became "lower the numbers behind the table's back". "Say what each point bought, out loud" became "Name what each point bought". "Say its state out loud" became "Announce its state". The two "say so" lines became "tell the table" and "warn the table first". "rules genuinely do not cover" lost the adverb. "to everyone's permanent confusion including his own" became "to everyone's bafflement, his own included" (the phrase's home is 07).
- **Keeps.** These earn their place:
  - exactly: "stop exactly where the text stops" (the read-aloud instruction), "(and he does, exactly once, …)" (a count), "Mother Sella can say exactly how out of date", "They are exactly as dangerous as their terms".
  - the whole: "the sentence the whole ball existed for", "worth the whole divergence".
  - out loud and say so: "The sergeant surrenders the field … and says so out loud" (a rules trigger at the gate).
  - genuinely: "When the table genuinely diverges".
- **Staccato rejoined.** Only scaffolding pairs were joined, using a colon, a semicolon or ", and". Punches that carry a fact or a beat stay: "Midnight. Bells.", "Then east.", "He stops.", "Make it count.", "The Bought's hours are over. The night is.", "Let them.", "Never confirm it.", "Guilt is slow, and slow is hallways."
- **DM Notes (O39).** "The table that will not stop trying to kill them" is now three plain paragraphs. Its "dial" was a redirect and its "cost" was a no-fudging warning, not a real setting with a tradeoff. "Which knives to show" keeps **Default / The dial / The cost** because its dial (show the combat-friendly snakes early) has a real cost (beats taken from the attack).
- **Emphasis italics are gone.** "*steer*", "*Nobody rolls Initiative yet.*" and "*crushes it in his fist*" are now roman. The Attendant states "*locked on you*" and "*drifting*" are DM speech and now sit in quotation marks. The model line for planting the Radiant's doubt ("no god worth the name …") is now in quotation marks too. Trait, spell, item and stat-block italics stay, along with the contract's Second Clause text.

# Oraga Night 5e — Inventions for Owner Review

Every new fictional fact (and every mechanical choice that reads like one) the 5e
edition had to make. Nothing here is settled until the owner approves, amends, or
strikes it. One table, numbered once; rows that four drafters logged separately for
the same fact have been merged, with every file that uses the fact listed.

## Review These First

These touch canon closest, or would be the most work to undo:

- **#2** — the Cousin's Blades are Boranis blood kin from 3160 (protects *House Boranis hired none*)
- **#9** — S7's clock lets Callun learn there may be an heir (the only snake beat that brushes the pregnancy secret)
- **#11** — Draunel's three other irons named, including the terrace duel and the arrest in the fire
- **#13** — Essin could trade the location of the missing year's two bodies
- **#17** — the Uninvited as 5e creatures: CR, *Humanoid (Human)*, *Leashed*, Fractures as rules
- **#18** — Master Vell's non-block
- **#1** — every faction brought a retinue *inside* the ball
- **#21** — which 5e classes are Thaumaturgy and which are Invocation
- **#23** — no spell scrolls in Val'loh
- **#26** — Veier's Thenya flare, in numbers

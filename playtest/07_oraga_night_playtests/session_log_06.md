## Scene: The Outer Gates of House Boranis (Arrival)

**Cyrus (MM):** Sleet falls over the dark slate roofs of Rekuzan. Before you loom the great bronze gates of House Boranis, silent for two years but tonight thrown wide. Warded light-crystals glow with warm amber fire, casting long shadows across the cobblestones. Guests in silk robes and silver-threaded cloaks queue before the entrance, their faces hidden behind elaborate spirit-masks.
A tall guard in heavy bronze armor steps forward, his sentinel mask reflecting the amber glow. He holds his hand out for your invitations.
What do you do?

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Serane steps forward with elegant posture, her porcelain Weaver mask catching the crystal light. 'Serane Vaskarin and entourage. We bring invitations stamped by Minister Corval himself.'"
>
> **Penny (Pello):** "I'll slide up beside her, adjusting my fox mask. 'And I am Pello, factor's representative from the Merchant's Circle. Here are our papers, guard.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent. The guard inspects the invitations. Because Serane is an Orthaen noble of a prominent house and Pello has legitimate business, this is a **Standard** difficulty check. Serane, you are speaking for the group. Roll Charisma + Persuade. You are spending a Spark to make a good impression, so you roll 3d6 and keep the highest two. Add your Charisma (+1) and Persuade (+1) modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Persuade roll (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 4, 3 -> keep 6 and 4) + 2 = **12** -> capped at **10** on server (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 10. That's a full success. The guard bows deeply, the bronze plates of his collar clicking. "Welcome, Lady Vaskarin. House Boranis is honored by your presence. Please, pass through the Court of Crystals. May the Weaver guide your steps tonight."
He waves his spear, and the inner gate swings open with a silent, oiled glide.
Dassa, Ilesse, as you walk behind Serane, what are you looking for?

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa walks with her hand resting loosely on the pommel of her greatsword. Her sentinel mask is neutral, but her eyes are scanning the guard layout, the gate hinges, and the exits. She grunts. 'Ten guards at the gate. More on the gallery. Raunu isn't throwing a party; he's securing a fortress.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** That's a Finesse check to observe the guard patterns without drawing attention. Roll Dexterity + Combat. Since it's a security layout, this is **Standard** difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Perception check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 4) + 1 = **10** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 10. Full success. Dassa, you notice that the guards aren't looking at the guests' invitations to verify their names; they are looking at the *masks*. They are counting the Orthaen crystal charges carried in the cloaks. You also spot a hidden side passage warded by minor crystal-runes that leads directly into the kitchens. You gain a **+1 forward modifier** if you need to use or bypass that passage later.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse watches the guests. 'The energy is... tense. People are laughing, but they glance at the sky. Cyrus, do I feel any spiritual echo or Attune vibration from the stone path?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Make a Wisdom + Attune roll. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Gate Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Attune check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 4) + 2 = **10** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 10. Full success! Ilesse, you attune to the cobblestones. You feel a rhythmic vibration hum through your boot soles. It's not a normal ward-hum. It's the high-frequency vibration of a **Threat Clock** ticking. It feels like the entire palace is humming on a timer, winding down toward midnight.
You step into the Crystal Court. The ballroom floor is a sheet of black obsidian reflecting two hundred masks and a thousand candles.

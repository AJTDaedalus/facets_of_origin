## Scene: The Audience Hall (Movement III - The Summons)

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, so Minister Corval finds you guys in the court and says: "The Master wishes to see you in the Audience Hall. Privately." He leads you through warded corridors to a quiet, cold room.
Raunu Boranis is sitting behind a stone desk. He is wearing a dark mask. He looks at you. "Why are you at my ball?"
Uh, who wants to speak?

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "I'll speak. Ilesse steps forward, bowing politely. 'We came to celebrate the Oraga harvest, Lord Boranis. And to witness the return of your house to Rekuzan's light.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Raunu is very suspicious. This is a Hard check (-1). Roll Charisma + Persuade.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Summons check (Hard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 1 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 7. Partial success. Raunu grunts. "I see. But your hawk mask is of the Kethaun border branch. Your family trades with the Thenya. You carry a message from my wife's uncle, Maiven Nolonaire. Tell me what it is."
Uh, you succeed in talking to him, but he knows your secret. You take a -1 penalty to your next roll.

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I'll try to intervene. Serane bows. 'My Lord, my cousin speaks for her family, but I speak for House Vaskarin. We are simply interested in the security of the trade lanes. The rumors of a new trade decree have made the Merchant's Circle anxious. We wanted to confirm it is fair.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1) to negotiate with him about the decree. Roll Intelligence + Persuade.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Summons check (Hard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 3) + 1 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 7. Raunu nods slowly. "The Tithe of Hands is fair. It levies a contribution from each sect to pay the festival laborers. But since you asked, you must sign a scroll promising that House Vaskarin will support the decree when it is read. If you don't, I will have you escorted out of the palace."
So you learn about the decree, but the cost is signing the scroll.

> **Penny (Pello):** "Wait! Don't sign it yet. Pello steps forward, showing his merchant guild credentials. 'My Lord, the Merchant's Circle simply wanted to discuss the details first. Surely we can reach an understanding without threats?'"

**Arthur (MM):** Pello, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Hard check (-1).

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Summons check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 0 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 7. Raunu turns to Pello. "A Phern merchant factor's nephew. I will speak to your uncle, Pello. But for now, you must leave your merchant seal on my desk as collateral. You can have it back at midnight. That's the cost."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa grunts. 'And my sister, Anha? She is in your kitchens. Let her come home for the festival.'"

**Arthur (MM):** Hard check (-1) for Dassa. Roll Strength + Combat.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Summons check (Hard difficulty, +0 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 0 = **7** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** A 7. Raunu looks at Dassa. "Anha is safe. She is working the east wing. You may see her, but only for five minutes, and you will be escorted by two guards. That's the deal."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "We accept. We have to work with what we can get."

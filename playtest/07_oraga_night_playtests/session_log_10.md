## Scene: The East Wing Vault (Movement VII - The Crossing)

**Cyrus (MM):** Sleet beats against the leaded windows of the east wing. Behind you, the ballroom is in flames; ahead, the warded corridor leads to the river gate. Veier Nolonaire (the bride) is sitting on the stone stairs, holding her newborn baby wrapped in a wadded silk cloak. She is bleeding, but her eyes are bright.
The Radiant (TR 12) floats down the gallery, its body crackling with blue electrical fire. It prepares to launch a lightning storm down the narrow corridor to incinerate Veier and her child.
What do you do?

> **Sophia (Serane):** "I have studied the Boranis wards and we found the Root of the House! Serane raises her Vaskarin light-crystal, channeling its light through the warded stone path. 'By the Weaver's lattice, lock the crystal valves! Raise the Crystal Barrier!' I want to cast a Warding spell to slide the warded crystal doors shut, locking the Radiant out."

**Cyrus (MM):** Excellent. Because you studied the Boranis wards and found the Root, this is a **Standard** difficulty check (normally Hard for non-Boranis). Serane, you are spending a Spark. Roll 3d6 and keep the highest two. Add Mind (+1) + Lore (+1) = +2 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Cast Warding]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Cast Warding magic (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 5, 4 -> keep 6 and 5) + 2 = **13** -> capped at **12** on server (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 12! Full success. The stone corridor clicks. Two massive, pale-green Weaver crystal doors slide out from the walls, locking together with a deep, resonant *THUD*. The Radiant's lightning storm splashes uselessly against the green crystal, illuminating the hallway in a flash of amber and emerald. The Radiant is locked on the other side.
But the Wept (TR 18) shadow-steps *through* the shadow of the pillar, appearing at the head of the stairs! It raises its hand to strike.

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse steps between the Wept and Veier. 'Lady Nolonaire, run!' I want to cast a Resonance spell, vibrating the air to invoke the Wept's **Fracture (Sorrow)**. I know she was a mother who lost her family; I want to play the cradle-song I heard in the nursery, using my resonance to hum the melody."

**Cyrus (MM):** This is a beautiful narrative move. Because you gathered the cradle-song clue in the nursery during Movement II, you have the necessary ammunition. Invoking a Fracture is a Soul-facet action. Because it is a boss, it is normally Hard, but you are spending a Spark. Roll 3d6, keeping the two highest. Add Spirit (+1) + Attune (+1) = +2 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Cast Resonance]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Cast Resonance magic (Standard difficulty due to Fracture ammunition, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 4, 3 -> keep 6 and 4) + 2 = **12** -> capped at **12** on server (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** A 12! Full success. The soft, humming cradle-song echoes through the stone archway. The Wept stops. Her hand, raised to strike, freezes in mid-air. Under the carved tears of her mask, a soft, human sigh escapes. For one full beat, she is just a mother standing in a burning palace, remembering what she lost. She does not move.
"Go," she whispers.
Raunu Boranis, wounded but alive, stumbles down the stairs. "Veier... the child..."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa grabs Raunu's shoulder, hauling him up. 'We go together. Pello, open the gate!'"
>
> **Penny (Pello):** "I use the runic schematic from the Root to unlock the river gate. It swings open!"

**Cyrus (MM):** You run out onto the river dock. Master Vell's rowboat is waiting in the sleet. You climb in, pushing off into the dark mist-tides of the Lake of Sorrows as the Boranis palace burns behind you.
Against all recorded history, both Raunu Boranis and his family have survived the night. You have broken history.
The session ends.

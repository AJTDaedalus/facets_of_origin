> **How to use this.** Read the last column before the session, not during it. Every

### Crisis Panel (Movement VI)

**Trigger:** mid-speech, on the dais, as the promised announcement begins.
**Assignments:** Wept → Raunu (B2 dais). Radiant → Veier (B9 → private stair → B5:
**the Crossing**, where Vell meets it — the crossfire hazard, Ch. V: DC 13 Dexterity
save each beat; fail = 7 (2d6) or lose an action shielding someone; fail by 5+ = 14
(4d6) or the ground gets worse). Hollow → main doors (B2), herding. Vell → service
passages → garden stair → river gate (B5).

**You cannot beat them** (*Leashed*, Ch. X): 0 HP ≠ kill. It throws aside whoever
struck the blow, steps into shadow, and walks back on at full HP. Anyone they drop
who is not their quarry is **Stable** and out of the scene — thrown clear, alive.
Force buys time; only ward-fire, the leash, and Fractures change outcomes. The spell
list does not change this; their blocks answer it spell by spell.

**The palace fights back:** barriers seal corridors; light floods stairwells; wards
over huddled guests. Steering a ward: an action and DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) for
the Agenda 5 player or anyone who read the wards by hand tonight, DC 18 for anyone
else. Within 30 ft. of an Uninvited, a steered ward holds 1 round, never longer.

**Crystals near an Uninvited:** within 30 ft., releasing a charge takes a DC 13
Charisma check; on a failure it goes dark, and is spent.

**Fractures (Ch. V — tell-table there):** Wept = sorrow (pages, chapel rite,
cradle-song, "my daughter") · Radiant = devotion (antique blessing, "whom do you
serve?"; deny the congregation or plant the doubt — guilt slows it; **it cannot be
turned**) · Hollow = despair (exits, flat answers; sincerity, not ferocity). An action
within 30 ft., an ability check with the skill the words fit; **1 tell = DC 18, 2+ =
DC 15**; Intimidation never works; once each.

**The leash:** ~half an hour of story. Late = faster, sloppier, more human. Ring the
last bell when the table needs the end.

**The Root (B11, if found):** master ward-valve; shelter for a dozen; the Uninvited
will not enter.

**The looters:** Tavva's crew hits the gallery (B7) and strips the fallen
(B3/corridors) — the night's one fully winnable fight, aimed at the players' better
natures (S5). They flee when the price turns real; a captive is dawn's best evidence
(Ch. V).

**The snakes in the dark:** every alert rule is suspended. Each faction's midnight
step is on the Snake Tracker above and in Ch. IX. Show them; let the party walk past
or walk in.

**Canonical pillars:** Raunu falls (his last charge seals doors behind Veier all the
way down) · Veier + Vell out the river gate · the Uninvited leave no trace · no one is
ever charged. Divergence sidebars: Chapter V.

# Facet of the Body

The Body is the Facet of people who get things done in person: the soldier at the door, the thief on the roof, the brawler who took the tavern's side in an argument about the tavern. It has the biggest hit die, every weapon and every armor, and three features that keep it on its feet. Mordai is Body. So is Zulnut, which tells you how wide the Facet is.

Body has no magic tradition of its own. A Body character can still learn magic through Spell talents from the Mind or Soul menus (*Body and Magic*, below).

## Body Features

Every Body character has these. They cost no pick and need no depth.

### Second Wind
*Body feature · 1st level*

As a bonus action, regain 1d10 + your level hit points. You have one use (two from 5th level). One use returns on a short rest, and all of them on a long rest.

### Action Surge
*Body feature · 2nd level*

Once per short rest, take one additional action on your turn. You can't use it to cast a spell.

### Indomitable
*Body feature · 9th level*

Once per long rest, when you fail a saving throw, you can reroll it and add your level to the new roll.

Seven Steel talents and three general ones. To read an entry, see *Reading a talent entry* in Chapter 02.

### Rage
*Steel talent · Body*

As a bonus action, if you aren't wearing heavy armor, you rage until the fight ends. While raging you resist bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage, deal +2 damage with Strength weapon attacks, have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saves, and can't cast spells or concentrate. Other talents' abilities still work (they aren't spells; Chapter 02). While raging you can also attack recklessly on your first attack of a turn: you have advantage on Strength melee attacks this turn, and attacks against you have advantage until your next turn. Whenever you wear no armor, your AC is 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier (a shield still counts). You have two rages; one returns on a short rest, all on a long rest.

**5th (Steel 2):** three rages. Once per long rest, when you would drop to 0 hit points while raging, you drop to 1 instead.
**9th (Steel 3):** four rages, and the damage bonus is +3.

### Martial Arts
*Steel talent · Body*

While you wear no armor and carry no shield, you gain these benefits. Your unarmed strikes and your simple or light melee weapons can use Dexterity instead of Strength, and their damage die is at least a d4. After you take the Attack action, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action. Your speed rises by 10 feet. Your AC is 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier.

You have focus points equal to half your level; all of them return on a short rest. Spend 2 for **Flurry** (two bonus-action unarmed strikes instead of one), or 1 for **Patient Defense** (Dodge as a bonus action) or **Step of the Wind** (Dash and Disengage as a bonus action).

**5th (Steel 2):** the damage die is at least a d6. **Stunning Strike:** once per turn when you hit, you can spend 1 focus point; the target makes a Constitution save against 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier or is stunned until the start of your next turn.
**9th (Steel 3):** the damage die is at least a d8.

### Cunning
*General talent · Body*

You can Dash, Disengage or Hide as a bonus action.

**5th:** when an attacker you can see hits you, you can use your reaction to halve the damage.
**9th:** when a Dexterity save would halve damage, you take none on a success and half on a failure.

### Guardian
*Steel talent · Body*

When a creature you can see hits an ally within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to take the hit instead, with everything it carries. Reduce its damage by half your level, rounded up, before resistance.

### Marksman
*Steel talent · Body*

As a bonus action, if you haven't moved this turn, your next ranged weapon attack this turn has advantage, and your speed is 0 until the turn ends.

**5th (Steel 2):** +2 to ranged weapon attack rolls.
**9th (Steel 3):** your ranged weapon attacks score a critical hit on a 19 or 20.

### Cleave
*Steel talent · Body*

Once per turn, when you hit with a heavy or versatile melee weapon, a second creature within 5 feet of both you and the target takes damage equal to your Strength modifier. There is no attack roll, and it isn't a rider.

**5th (Steel 2):** the second creature takes your Strength modifier + your proficiency bonus.

## Body and Magic

Body has no tradition of its own. A Body character can still take Spell talents from the Mind or Soul menus, as cross-Facet picks (at most two). With the first one, name **Invocation** or **Thaumaturgy**: that is your tradition from then on, and you cast with its ability (the higher of Wisdom and Charisma for Invocation, Intelligence for Thaumaturgy) and choose your domains from its list.

Because Body counts two extra Steel talents when finding its main track (not for depth), Steel is always your main track. Your first Spell talent gives you Spellcasting and the Half table; Full Casting and Deep Magic stay out of reach. A Body character can learn enough magic to be a warrior with spells. It can't become a full caster.

**Which Spell talent?** With two to four slots at most, pick one that works without them: *Mending Hands* (a healing pool), *Channel* (one heal or harm a short rest), or *Turn the Odds* (Sparks). *Evoker* rewards a caster with slots to spend, and a Body character won't have many. With Steel depth 1 or more, your weapon or shield can be your focus (Chapter 07), so you needn't sheathe your sword to cast.

## Example: Zulnut Skips the Cards

Zulnut's player looks at the Monk card and puts it back.

**Zulnut:** *Martial Arts*. He'd rather not carry a weapon, and armor is heavy.

**MM:** And at 3rd?

**Zulnut:** *Cunning*. Bonus-action Disengage. He hits someone and leaves.

**MM:** (The Monk card takes *Cunning* at 3rd too. He has built the Monk while refusing to be the Monk.)

→ Zulnut is Steel 1 at 1st level: *Martial Arts* is his one Steel talent, so he has Martial Weapons, and Steel is his main track. *Cunning* is general, so it adds no depth; if he wants *Extra Attack* at 5th, his 5th-level pick has to be a second Steel talent.

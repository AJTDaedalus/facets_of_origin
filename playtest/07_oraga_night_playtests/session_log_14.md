## Scene: The Crystal Court (Movement VI - Midnight Ballroom Combat)

**Arthur (MM):** The lights fail. Slaver lashes whip out of the darkness. The Hollow (TR 7) blocks the door, accompanied by two Gallery Knives (TR 3 each). They are looting the guests who have fallen to the floor.
Let's enter combat. Everyone declare your postures. Dassa is Defensive. Pello is Measured. Ilesse is Measured. Serane is Measured.
Dassa, what is your action?

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa strikes the first Gallery Knife to stop it from robbing an old priest. I want to keep the mooks off Pello."

**Arthur (MM):** Pello, what do you do?

> **Penny (Pello):** "I want to strike the same Gallery Knife to double-team it!"

**Arthur (MM):** Okay, so Pello and Dassa both strike the same Gallery Knife.
Let's resolve Pello's strike. Roll Dexterity + Combat. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Strike Mook]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (4, 3) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Pello hits the Gallery Knife. As a Mook, a partial success means you hit him, but he lashes back at you.
Now let's resolve Dassa's strike. Roll Strength + Combat. In Defensive posture, Dassa takes a -1 penalty, so +1 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Strike Mook]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Strike roll (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (3, 4) + 1 = **8** (Partial Success).

**Arthur (MM):** An 8. Partial success. Dassa hits him too, but because it's a partial success, the scout lashes back at Dassa as well.
Wait, since both of you got a partial success, both of you take a strike back from the Gallery Knife!
Pello, you must react. Dodge costs 1. Roll Dexterity.

> **Penny (Pello):** "I Dodge!"

*(Note: The server script did not resolve reactions because the mook was defeated by the strikes, but Arthur adjudicates it as a messy, simultaneous exchange.)*

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Wait, Arthur. The Gallery Knife is a Mook (TR 3). Between Dassa's strike and Pello's strike, we hit it twice. A single strike is enough to defeat a Mook. It should be destroyed before it can strike both of us back, or at least one of the strikes should neutralize it."
>
> **Arthur (MM):** "Uh... right, let me check page 48. Yes, Mooks are defeated in one strike. But since both were partial successes, I'll say you defeat the scout, but you get covered in warded crystal-shatter smoke. Pello, you are 'Staggered' (Tier 1). Dassa, you are fine due to your heavy armor.
> But the Hollow is still at the door, and the second Gallery Knife is slipping away with the bag of loot.
> The exchange ends."

## Scene: The Audience Hall (Movement III - The Summons)

**Cyrus (MM):** The Audience Hall is vast, warded by ancient, pale-green Weaver crystals that thrum with a heavy, protective pulse. Raunu Boranis sits behind his stone desk. He is not looking at documents; he is looking at his hands, which are steady. His mask is off, revealing a face that is tired but completely focused.
"Lady Vaskarin. Pello. Dassa. Ilesse. I know what you have been asking in my halls. The Tithe of Hands. The river gate. My sister. My wife.
Let us speak of the truth. Why are you here?"

> **Sophia (Serane):** "Serane curtsies gracefully. 'Lord Boranis, my house wants to support yours. But we must understand your designs. The silence of House Boranis has made Rekuzan uneasy. If we know the truth, we can defend you in the Circle.'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Raunu looks at Serane. "You speak of support, Serane. But you carry a Vaskarin soul-crystal. You want to see the Root of my house."
This is a Standard difficulty persuasion check because Serane spent a Spark, rolls 3d6, and has a +2 modifier.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Serane Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Serane
*   **Action:** Summons check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier, 1 Spark spent)
*   **Roll:** 3d6 (6, 5, 2 -> keep 6 and 5) + 2 = **13** -> capped at **11** on server (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 11. Full success. Raunu stands. "Very well. If you seek the truth, you shall see it. The Root of the House is not a secret; it is a weight." He touches a rune on his desk. The stone floor slides open, revealing a stair leading to the vault below. "The Root is the great crystal matrix that stabilizes the mist-tides of Rekuzan. House Boranis holds the master valve. From there, the crystal wards can be steered palace-wide. Go down. Look upon it."

> **Penny (Pello):** "Pello looks down the stairs. 'Wow. That's a lot of magic. Lord Boranis, is it true that the river gate is warded from there too?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Pello, roll Wisdom + Persuade. Standard difficulty.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Pello Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Pello
*   **Action:** Summons check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 1 = **11** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 11! Full success. Raunu nods. "Yes, Pello. The river gate is linked. Master Vell has the key, but from the Root, you can bypass the lock entirely and open it. Here, take this runic schematic. It will guide you."

> **Luke (Dassa):** "Dassa grunts. 'And Anha?'"

**Cyrus (MM):** Raunu looks at Dassa. "Anha is warded in the east wing with Veier. She is there by choice, Dassa. Go to her. Protect them both."
This is a Standard difficulty show of respect check. Dassa rolls.

### [Mechanical Resolution: Dassa Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Dassa
*   **Action:** Summons check (Standard difficulty, +1 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 5) + 1 = **11** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 11. Full success. Raunu gives Dassa a heavy brass token. "This passes the east wing wards. Keep them safe."

> **Toby (Ilesse):** "Ilesse bows. 'Thank you, Lord Boranis. We will.'"

### [Mechanical Resolution: Ilesse Summons Check]
*   **Player:** Ilesse
*   **Action:** Summons check (Standard difficulty, +2 modifier)
*   **Roll:** 2d6 (5, 4) + 2 = **11** (Full Success).

**Cyrus (MM):** An 11. Full success! Raunu looks at Ilesse. "Tell Maiven Nolonaire that my wife is hers, and she is mine. We have chosen this night."
He waves his hand, and you descend into the Root of the House.

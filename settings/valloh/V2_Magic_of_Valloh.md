# V2. The Magic of Val'loh

Val'loh has the same magic as the rest of the game. Same 2d6, same Domain plus Intent plus Scope, same difficulty by scope, same Sparks. There is no slot, no per-day count, and no tempo rule. If you know how to cast in Facets of Origin, you know how to cast here.

What differs is only **where a domain comes from**, and there are two answers.

---

## Gifts

A Gift is a domain carried in the blood (Chapter II.5). Intuitive tradition — Spirit and Attune. Focused, every one of them, because a people's magic does one thing deeply. Minor scope until it formalizes at your first Facet level, and full scope after.

That is the whole of it. A Gift is not a lesser magic and not a special case; the Bestiary resists it exactly the way it resists a Soul mage's Shadow, and an Orthaen sealing a corridor is making the same kind of decision about scope and difficulty that any caster makes.

The ten Gift domains are the ten lineages' (`V1`), and they are catalogued at the end of this chapter in the same format the core's Magic Domain appendix uses: territory, what lies beyond the focus, and three example intents at each scope.

**You cannot learn one.** A Gift never appears on a Technique's list of domains to choose from. There is no course of study that ends in a Val'loh gift, and no Soul mage in Shattered Origin can decide to take up Crystal. This is the one place the setting is stricter than the core, and it is a restriction on *acquisition*, not on use.

---

## Spellcraft

The other answer is study, and it works as Chapter II.3 already writes it: the scholarly tradition, Knowledge and Lore, from the Mind list of domains, at the standard cross-Facet cost for anyone whose Facet is not Mind.

The fiction of Val'loh says spellcraft is a patient art. A spellform is drawn thread by thread; the useful ones take a scholar hours, and the great ones take seasons; the histories are full of workings that collapsed because someone tried to finish one in a hurry. Every word of that is true in the world.

**None of it is a rule.** A spellform caster in play rolls what any caster rolls, as often as the fiction allows, at the difficulty their scope sets. The patience is where the fiction lives — in what a character has spent their life on, in why the Church guards its scholars, in why nobody in a burning hall is drawing threads — and the difficulty ladder is where the game lives.

A gifted member of a tribe may learn spellcraft as anyone may. Nothing about the blood interferes with the study, and several of the more irritating figures in Val'loh's history have had both.

---

## Crystal

Orthaen crystal holds finished workings. This is the fact the whole economy of Val'loh rests on, and it is why the walls of Rekuzan are wealth and not decoration.

In play, a **charge** is a one-use item. One stored working, at one scope, released at a touch by anyone — gifted or not, trained or not. There is no roll.

That last part is the point. A charged crystal is magic that the ungifted can carry and spend, which is the only route to a working most people in Val'loh will ever have, and it is why a small pink splinter is worth a season's wages in the wrong district.

**Growing one** is an ordinary Crystal working, done in downtime, under the normal rules of Chapter II.3. Nothing extra is tracked: the character makes the roll their scope calls for, and if it lands they have a charge.

**Spending one** takes no roll at all. The charge is in the inventory or it is not. When the fiction makes a *release* chancy — fumbled in the dark, jostled in a crowd, held near something that eats magic — that is a Luck roll at Standard, which is a thing the table already knows how to do.

The six charges every gifted Orthaen has heard of are **steady light**, **a sealed door**, **a veil of quiet**, **a chime at a threshold**, **warmth**, and **a held image**. All Minor. All in the equipment list. A setting can grow the list; the rules do not change when it does.

> **MM Note — crystal and the things that eat it**
>
> **Default:** a charge releases when it is touched, and it works.
>
> **The dial:** you can rule that a specific thing in your scene *suppresses* charges — they gutter, they will not take, the light dies as it leaves the stone.
>
> **The cost:** almost nothing, and it is the best tool this setting hands you. It is a legible, physical signal that something is badly wrong, and it costs a player no resource to receive. Use it where you would otherwise have narrated dread. Do not use it so often that players stop growing charges.

# The Gift Domains

Ten entries, in the core appendix's format. All are **Focused** — Easy at Minor, Standard at Significant, Hard at Major — and all use the **intuitive** tradition: Spirit, plus the Attune rank.

The example intents are what the scope ladder *feels like* for that gift. They are illustrations, not a list of permitted effects; a player's intent is whatever they describe, and the scope is the MM's call about how much of the world it moves.

> **Reading the Entries — Gift Domains**
>
> **Territory** — what the gift is about.
>
> **Beyond this domain's focus** — what it is not about. Read this one twice. In a Focused domain the boundary does more work than the territory: it is what stops "Crystal" from quietly meaning "anything made of matter" by the third session.
>
> **Minor / Significant / Major** — three example intents each.

---

## Crystal *(Orthaen)*

**Territory:** Growing, shaping, charging, and reading soul-crystal; holding a working in a lattice.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** light that is not crystal-light; stone that was never grown; and any working the crystal merely *holds* — that is the spellform's domain, not this one.

**Minor:** Wake a wall's stored warmth on a cold night · read whether a charge is live without touching it · grow a splinter of crystal to mark a door you must find again in the dark.

**Significant:** Seal a corridor with a lattice grown across it in the time it takes to say so · steer a palace ward to light one stairwell and not another · draw the light out of a room's crystal so the room goes dark.

**Major:** Raise a ward that holds a hall for a scene against anything that is not deep crystal itself · grow, over a night, a charge at Major scope · read a generations-old lattice back to its maker's intent.

---

## Warning *(Phern)*

**Territory:** Sensing hidden danger, its direction, and the intent behind it, before it lands.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** seeing the unseen — this is a prickle, not sight; reading a specific mind; and danger nobody has decided on yet.

**Minor:** Know which door in a hall you should not open · feel the moment a conversation turns hostile · wake before the thief reaches the tent.

**Significant:** Walk a crowded room and point to the three people who mean harm tonight · feel an ambush's shape well enough to name where it waits · sense that the danger in the palace is *upstairs*, and that it is not human.

**Major:** Hold the whole caravan's watch alone for a night, waking each sleeper the instant they are needed · feel a threat coming from a day away, and the road it takes · know, for one held breath, exactly where every knife in a burning hall is.

---

## The Bond *(Thenya)*

**Territory:** Protective intervention for someone the caster loves, fired by feeling rather than technique.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** anyone the caster does not love; harm to the caster themselves; and anything premeditated — the Bond answers a moment, never a plan.

**Minor:** A loved one's stumble becomes a step · the cup they were about to drink from cracks · you know, across a city, that they are afraid.

**Significant:** The blade that would have opened them turns on the rib · the fall that would have broken them lands them winded · you are, for one exchange, standing where you were not.

**Major:** The wall between you and them is not there · the blow already narrated does not land, and the one who swung it does not understand why · the thread that was cut holds.

---

## Blade-bond *(Akathi)*

**Territory:** One bonded weapon: finding it, calling it, striking with it as a part of the self.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** any other weapon; armor; and anyone else wielding the bonded blade.

**Minor:** The blade is in your hand before you reached for it · know where it lies from a room away · it does not rust, dull, or lose its edge between fights.

**Significant:** Call the blade back across a hall · parry a blow nobody saw coming, because the blade did · put it through the one gap in a guard you have been watching all fight.

**Major:** The blade goes through what should have stopped it — a ward, a crystal barrier, a door · it finds its wielder across any distance in the world · one cut ends a duel that was never going to end any other way.

---

## Stone-flesh *(Dekhi)*

**Territory:** Enduring — cold, poison, hunger, exhaustion, and blows that would drop another.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** healing others; strength as such — this is lasting, not lifting; and anything that requires speed.

**Minor:** Walk a winter night in shirtsleeves · shrug off a dose that would sicken a soldier · go a second day without sleep and show nothing.

**Significant:** Hold a collapsing doorway with your back for the exchange it takes everyone to get through · keep walking through a blizzard that has stopped the column · take the hit meant for the person behind you and stay standing.

**Major:** Stand in the fire long enough to carry four people out of it · outlast a poison meant to kill a village · be the last thing between a thing and a door, for as long as that has to be true.

---

## Dream *(Kshalo)*

**Territory:** Entering and shaping the dreams of the sleeping.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** the waking mind; anyone the caster has not met or does not hold something truly theirs; and anything that leaves a physical mark.

**Minor:** Learn what a sleeper is afraid of tonight · leave a face in a stranger's dream that they will recognise tomorrow · sleep, and wake knowing whether someone you have met is alive.

**Significant:** Walk a named person's dream and ask it three questions, answered in dream-logic · plant a warning that survives waking · turn a nightmare aside.

**Major:** Hold a dream open for a whole night and bring another sleeper into it · leave a compulsion that acts on waking, once, in a way the dreamer can explain to themselves · walk the dream of someone you have never met, holding only their name and a thing they loved.

---

## Wildspeech *(Fthala)*

**Territory:** Speaking with, bonding with, and moving among animals and wild places.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** commanding a beast against its nature; plants, which are Verdance's; and anything inside a city's walls that is not an animal.

**Minor:** Ask a mews of falcons what frightened them · move through undergrowth without a sound · send a small companion beast with a message it understands.

**Significant:** The dogs of a whole estate decide the intruders are not you · a bear steps aside · every bird in the garden goes up at once, when you say so.

**Major:** The forest closes a road behind you for a day · every animal within a mile turns toward the thing that walked through it · a bonded beast dies for you, and you know it before it does.

---

## The Weave *(Scora)*

**Territory:** Perfect recall of anything witnessed or recorded; pattern across memory; one true question of the past.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** things never witnessed by anyone; the future; and changing what was recorded.

**Minor:** Recite a conversation heard once, verbatim · recognise a mask, a stitch, or a coin from a description · know which of two accounts contradicts the other.

**Significant:** Lay out every rumour heard tonight and see which three are the same rumour · read a room's history from the wear on its floor · ask the MM one true question about a past event, and be answered.

**Major:** Hold an entire night in memory so exactly that it can be sworn as testimony and *believed* · find the one thread that connects two houses across four centuries · reconstruct a destroyed record from every mind that ever read it.

---

## Mindshare *(Krenn — MM-side)*

**Territory:** Telepathy among kin; reading and, at scope, steering other minds.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** the dead; anyone shielded by deep crystal; and changing what someone *is* rather than what they do next.

**Minor:** Know a stranger's surface intent · go unremembered by one guard · speak to kin across a room without a word.

**Significant:** Be unmemorable to a hall · read the fear in every mind in a room · nudge a weak will toward the door.

**Major:** Edit two hundred minds' attention for a night · hold a mind still · take a decision away from someone and leave them certain it was theirs.

*Not a player option in 3164. The Krenn are gone, and this entry exists so the one who is not can be run honestly.*

---

## Resonance of Stone *(Tyndi — MM-side)*

**Territory:** Metals and stones that carry and release magical power; gadgets and their control gems.

**Beyond this domain's focus:** crystal grown by the Orthaen gift; living things; and anything without a stone or a metal in it.

**Minor:** Wake a device from its control gem · feel a vein of ore through a wall · make a knife hum.

**Significant:** Run a machine nobody has run in a generation · ground a spellform's power into a nexus stone · make a tool do a thing it was not built for.

**Major:** Light a fort's weave-furnace · awaken every device in a hall at once · smelt a gem that lets the ungifted command what only the gifted could.

> **Through the Mirror — why none of these entries carries a rule**
>
> Three of the drafted gifts originally did. Blade-bond let its Strike leave an enemy Open on a 7–9 as well as a 10+, and resolved as a Final Blow once per session at Major scope. Stone-flesh let its holder take a Tier 2 Condition as Tier 1 once per scene, and refuse to be Broken once per session. All of them are cut, and none of them is in the catalog above.
>
> Not because they were bad ideas — because we had already measured what happens when a gift carries a rule. The Thenya Bond was drafted with a reaction clause of exactly this shape, and simulation put the Hard encounter row at 75% party victory against a 40–60% band. One lineage's blood was worth more than a whole difficulty step.
>
> A domain that grants an extra Condition tier, a free reaction, or a second Final Blow is not a domain any more; it is a Technique that skipped the Technique economy, handed out at creation, to a character who paid nothing for it. If a gift should do one of those things, it should be a **Technique** in a Facet tree where it costs a pick, and it should be simulated before it prints.
>
> What the gifts get instead is territory — which in this system is the larger gift. *The blade goes through what should have stopped it* does not need a rule attached. It needs an MM who reads it and calls the difficulty.
